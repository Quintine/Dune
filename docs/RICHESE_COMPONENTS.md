# Richese physical card collection audit

Audited 6 September 2026. The ten physical identities and their readable face instructions are verified below. This is an isolated inventory and original gameplay guide, **not runtime activation or certification of combined interactions**. No general deck factory, selectable expansion or battle resolver was activated. The checkpoints below distinguish read-only presentation from the later development-only auction integration.

## Stone Burner, complete ordinary portraits and recovery checkpoint (2026-09-06)

- Canonical Stone Burner now uses the weapon slot without changing its saved `kind: special` identity. It has a named Voice role, Prescience/Truth-compatible original commitments, a private precommit availability check, and an event-bound owner choice after both plans reveal. Kill mode kills both participating leader discs; ignore mode preserves other weapon attacks. Both replace ordinary strength with undialed physical-token comparison, with the aggressor winning ties. Neither adds the Kwisatz Haderach bonus or kills that companion. Normal support, bounty, traitor/explosion precedence, casualty choices and winner/loser/Moritani retention remain shared with the existing battle flow.

- Physical counting enumerates every legal casualty allocation. A winner is accepted only when every permitted pair agrees. The printed Advanced Emperor example includes three possible remaining-token totals; where all give the same winner, the winner still chooses its normal casualty allocation afterward. Where the choice could change the winner, a conservative check rejects Stone before either hidden opposing plan is inspected. Foreign No-Field values are represented by public possibilities, never read to decide availability. Compulsory Stone Voice must leave a common zero-spice completion across those public possibilities.

- Two actual interpretation questions remain unanswered: the commitment procedure when Advanced or Basic Ix physical allocations change the winner, and the priority of opposing Stone/Poison Tooth revealed choices. No policy was inferred from silence. A public Ix-configuration guard prevents accepting a Stone plan whose combined timing cannot yet finish. This is an explicit implementation boundary, not a printed prohibition. Mirror/copied weapons and unfinished modules remain uncertified. See [STONE_BURNER_ENGINE_AUDIT.md](STONE_BURNER_ENGINE_AUDIT.md).

- Player controls show both original plans, exact undialed counts or honest possible totals, the mode choice and a complete internal guide. No victim/death confirmation is added. The card stays physically reserved until standard cleanup; stale events, duplicate requests and corrupted source custody fail before resources change. All four AI profiles use public force possibilities, legal low-dial plans and revealed leader/bounty information. They preserve leader value when Artillery suppresses bounty and leave traitor/explosion precedence intact.

- Semuta Drug, Mirror Weapon and Juice of Sapho remain inactive. Source audits now identify Semuta's fresh-discard continuation requirements and Sapho's scoped ordering requirements; these are not runtime completion claims. Other unfinished faction/module systems, mobile acceptance, difficulty-strength calibration and complete Advanced/expansion games still prevent goal completion. No start gate was removed.

## Shared custody, Basic / Advanced and privacy

1. Rulebook p5 places the ten cards in Richese’s separate cache, outside its hand. The factory creates only this physical inventory and does not add it to the ordinary deck. A collection card enters a hand through a valid acquisition; selling does not itself discard it.
2. Whenever actually discarded, a collection card goes into the **normal discard pile** (p5), not back into the Richese cache. Ordinary later deck recycling therefore treats it as a normal discarded card unless a card effect intervenes. An all-zero cache-auction choice to remove a card is removal from the game, not discard.
3. Rulebook p4 keeps discard contents private unless an effect permits inspection. Nullentropy grants its user a secret search; Semuta is limited to a current eligible discard event. Neither gives every player a discard browser.
4. These ten cards belong to the normal Richese setup and their faces are not an Advanced-only module. Advanced play adds Richese Black Market and its special Karama acquisition (pay3 to choose a cached card secretly); it does not create a different ten-card inventory. The optional Leader Skill and Advanced Stronghold modules are separate and their interactions are not certified here.
5. Basic and Advanced share each face’s core instruction. Advanced interactions needing extra care include force support and physical tokens for Stone Burner, faction Voice/Prescience, leader skills/custody, late defenses and phase powers. `spiceCost: 0` in metadata means the **card itself** states no extra activation fee; it never makes shipping, purchasing or a chosen faction Karama power free. Nullentropy’s explicit activation fee is2.
6. A Richese ally may receive a Richese-family card already in Richese’s hand if the recipient has room (p6); this is not permission to give from cache. Distrans is its own broader player-to-player effect.
7. Acquisition origin must be stored separately from printed family. Ix’s ally cannot replace a direct cache purchase, but can replace a Black Market purchase even if its face is a Richese card (FAQ p10). Harkonnen bonus draw applies to either purchase (FAQ p11). Neither purchase route permits Karama acquisition (pp5–6).

## Isolated API and required next integration

- `RICHESE_CARD_DEFINITIONS`: immutable physical card, quantity, printed type, timing, summary/full guide, behavior and verification metadata.
- `richeseCards()`: fresh mutable inventory; no activation, hand access, deck access or random draw.
- `richeseCardDefinition(card)`: exact ID, name, kind and effect match; does not enrich impostor/similarly named cards.
- `RICHESE_COLLECTION`: separate-cache and discard-routing metadata, explicitly not implemented.

The existing `Card` union has no dedicated Stone Burner/Mirror/Portable types. This isolated module therefore uses `kind: 'special'` as a transport shape and records their **real printed battle categories separately**. It must not be fed to existing generic battle validators; those require dedicated legal-slot/effect integration. A later change should review and migrate that transport type before activating the collection. Existing base/Ix deck assembly and expansion gating remain unchanged.

Tests in `tests/richese-cards.test.ts` check exact composition, physical ID uniqueness against base/Ix, fresh custody objects, deep immutability, strict lookup, printed categories, normal/Box disposal, critical face restrictions and no silent deck activation. These are component-contract checks, not tests of the ten gameplay effects. No multiplayer or browser completion claim is made for Richese.
