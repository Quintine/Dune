## Connected mechanics and controls

The question packet is `{kind:'bribe',target,turn,recipient,minimum}` within the
original `truthAsk`; minimum is a positive safe integer. A real definite answer
retains the original declaration, priority, public reply, one physical discard
and interrupted phase continuation. A distinct event starts progress0. Public
text names the recipient. Only the respondent receives feasible answer choices,
its own promise records and its own next ordinary-payment guide.

The original native bribe action owns payer debit and the original deferred
recipient receipt. The promise records payer progress once; Mentat collection
does not pay or fulfill it again. Current unpaid bids and sealed battle support
stay reserved. Private compatibility quotations complete their own planned
payments before checking earlier battle, bid, revival, shipment and movement
obligations, without creating live receipts or spendable recipient cash.
An earlier bribe Yes also constrains a later supported typed answer.

The persistent panel shows named cumulative progress and fulfilled/released
status. Its guarded native bribe callback may act out of turn in a quiet legal
window. Current ordinary payment limits enforce each No cap and preserve funds
for other Yes answers; no separate legality calculation is installed in the UI.
Stale, wrong-owner or modified guided packets reject. Existing four minimal AI
profiles use feasible answers and the exact native guide. Strategy and difficulty
calibration remain deferred.

Own voluntary spending or an alliance offer to the unpaid named recipient cannot
evade Yes. Collection Ready requires its payment; a new Collection Yes reopens
that respondent's readiness. Pending consequences finish before forced release;
known own spending retains the promised current funds. Completed opposing
impossibility releases only incompatible Yes answers in accepted order. No ends
at the original turn boundary and never binds the next turn.
