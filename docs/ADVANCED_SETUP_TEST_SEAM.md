Implemented checkpoint, 7 September 2026. Scope: the six original factions, Basic/Advanced, two through six players, no expansions or optional modules. The shared initializer, persisted setup stages, private projections, bot stage guards and setup controls are implemented. **The public Advanced and expansion start gates remain closed.** This document supersedes its earlier proposed design; it does not certify complete Advanced readiness.

At this checkpoint the coordinator reports **1,446 registered unit/rules/client/component tests passed**, including the **456-case Advanced setup matrix**. ## Published ordering and historical defect

The official base rulebook, printed p18, places BG prediction **“After step 1 (Positions) in the Setup Phase”**. Printed p6 orders the normal setup as Positions, traitor dealing/selection, starting spice, force placement, starting Treachery Cards, then the turn marker. Advanced BG placement follows Fremen placement and becomes a fighter when alone. Prediction therefore precedes private traitor/treachery information, and normal treachery dealing follows force-placement choices. [GF9 base rulebook, pp6 and 18](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/Dune-Rulebook.pdf)

The official CHOAM rulebook's Leader Skills variant changes the usual order: positions and BG prediction precede early treachery and skill dealing. That confirms that early treachery is a specified variant change, which is not part of this no-module setup. [GF9 CHOAM & Richese, p8](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf)

The original source review used the publisher PDF/text `/tmp/dune-rules/base.pdf` and `.txt`, plus official-domain indexed results reproducing base p6/p18 and CHOAM p8. Its targeted November 2020 FAQ searches found no setup-prediction timing override. Direct fresh access to that FAQ returned HTTP403, so this is **not** a claim that every page of a newly downloaded FAQ was reread. This implementation update preserves those source facts and access limits; it does not claim new FAQ retrieval. [GF9 November 2020 FAQ](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf)

Before this correction, the initializer dealt both private pools and fixed resources before accepting prediction, traitor or variable force-placement inputs. BG could see its cards and Fremen's chosen distribution before predicting; players could choose traitors or force placements with knowledge of starting treachery. That was an information-order defect in Basic as well as Advanced. The older calibration artifacts remain unaltered historical evidence of that implementation's behavior, not evidence that its setup matched the published order.

## Implemented shared entry points

`game/engine.ts` now uses one private `initializeSetup` from both public `start` and the exported offline `initializeBaseGameForAudit(state: Game): Game`. The latter clones its input, validates a fresh isolated lobby, invokes the shared initializer and returns `normalizeAutomaticGame(g)`. It never flips an already initialized Basic game into Advanced.

The public lobby dispatcher still checks host authority. Public `start` still requires at least two players and all players ready, and rejects Advanced, expansion factions and selected expansions. Maximum-six and duplicate-faction protection on the public path continue to come from valid lobby construction; this document does not claim that the isolated private `start` function validates every possible corrupted roster. `newPlayer`, `createGame`, `joinGame`, circle-selection and readiness actions remain the constructors used by setup and full-game audit fixtures.

The offline seam separately validates two through six unique player IDs/factions, an existing host, all players ready, base-only factions, no selected expansion or tech-token module, valid owned player circles, lobby/turn/phase freshness, and no existing decks, dealt cards, forces, resources, prediction, typed inventory or pending game work to replace. It rejects repeated initialization and leaves its input unchanged on success or failure. These are concrete fresh-lobby checks, not a general validator for every imaginable corrupted historical state.

There is no player action, HTTP route, query flag or `allowUnsupported` field that dispatches the audit initializer. Forged fields on public `start` do not bypass the mode gate. Ordinary room actions continue through `applyAction`; tests and the offline Advanced calibration import the seam directly. Public Basic and audit Basic initialization are compared on the corrected code under the same seeded shuffle stream.

## Persisted stages and private information

`Game.setupStage` is optional and has exactly three values: `prediction`, `traitors`, and `forces`. These are setup substages, separate from the nine game phases. `GameView.setupStage` is null for non-setup and legacy rooms. Public `setupPending` contains only the IDs entitled to act at the current stage, never private card identities or prediction contents.

| Stage                | Automatic entry/continuation                                                                                             | Accepted input and information boundary                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `prediction`         | Fix circles, storm order/dialers and privately shuffle decks. Skip automatically if BG is absent.                        | Only BG locks its faction/turn prediction. Every hand, traitor pool and force placement is still empty, with starting spice unallocated. No rival receives the secret prediction.                                           |
| `traitors`           | Deal each participating faction its proper pool exactly once; Harkonnen automatically keeps its four.                    | Each remaining owner selects one of its own choices. Unused identities return to the traitor reserve. Hands remain empty; force-placement actions are rejected.                                                             |
| `forces`             | After every traitor choice, allocate printed starting spice, fixed forces and Advanced typed reserves.                   | Fremen places ten tokens among permitted sectors. Advanced BG acts only after seated Fremen completes placement; if Fremen is absent, BG can place immediately. Hands remain empty until every required placement finishes. |
| Automatic completion | Clear `setupStage`, deal normal starting hand quantities once, and continue through existing setup/first-Storm routines. | No deal-confirmation or stage-advance action is required. The first Storm input starts with the correctly private complete starting hands.                                                                                  |

Advanced Emperor receives five Sardaukar and Fremen three Fedaykin through the shared starting-force routine. Basic BG retains its fixed Polar Sink placement; Advanced BG receives its separate placement opportunity and normal stance settlement. The normal ten-turn marker is represented internally from game creation; this creates no early player choice. Privately shuffling undealt decks does not expose them through `viewGame`.

The authoritative setup dispatcher checks both action kind and entitled owner against the stage. All four bot profiles use the public stage before enumerating prediction, traitor and force candidates. They preserve the public BG prerequisite that seated Fremen must have completed its ten-force placement. The UI names the stage and waiting owners, hides premature controls, explains the BG wait, and says starting treachery remains undealt until force placement completes. Automatic steps have no extra confirmation control.

Legacy saved setup rooms without a stage keep their already-dealt hands and existing choice flow. They are not reset or redealt. Their already disclosed information cannot be made historically compliant by moving cards back into decks; the new order applies to newly initialized staged rooms. Non-setup saves similarly do not acquire a setup stage.
