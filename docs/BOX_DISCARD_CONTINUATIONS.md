## Completed transaction and continuation

Payment happens once before private inspection. Selection validates prospective transfers, commits the recovered card to its owner, shuffles the remaining discard pile once and places the activating canonical Box on top. Only that used Box creates a fresh public discard receipt. Older shuffled cards remain old discards, and the selected card is a private transfer.

The `nullentropyDiscard` continuation records the search owner/event, activating Box, private selected-card receipt, final ordered discard IDs/signature and suspended response, decision, pending Karama and phase opening. Those receipts are evidence, not extra physical card custody. The suffix retires the monotonic discard sequence before restoring only the four saved controls. It never charges, selects, shuffles, repeats the previous action or restores a whole game snapshot.

With one eligible target, staging and retirement occur in the original paid action without another acknowledgement. Multiple targets retain the existing owner-only paid decision. A restored Harkonnen bonus may legitimately refill the deck after the completed Box frame retires; that subsequent draw does not repeat the Box search.
