# Fremen ordinary revival audit

Audit date: 2026-09-06. Scope: Fremen purchases, Tleilaxu permission, La La La and Emperor-funded extras. This source audit makes no runtime edits and does not resolve the separate Recruits timing questions.

**Correction, later on 2026-09-06:** the original conclusion prohibiting Fremen purchases even with Tleilaxu is **retracted**. This audit initially missed the explicit Fremen answer on printed p. 5 of the official November 2020 FAQ. The author, coordinating agent and an independent reviewer have now retrieved that answer. The earlier reproduction remains below as historical evidence, with its interpretation corrected.

The successful targeted query included the exact FAQ question about Fremen buying more revived forces with Tleilaxu.

## Corrected conclusions

**With Tleilaxu present, Fremen may buy normal force revivals.** Their normal cap remains 3 unless valid Tleilaxu permission raises it to 5. Faction presence establishes the purchase exception; a separate permission establishes the higher cap. Enabling the Ix expansion without a Tleilaxu player does not establish the FAQ's stated condition. Pricing, available Tanks, prior usage and applicable cancellation still constrain the transaction. This combines the specific FAQ with the expansion's existing permission rule; the FAQ does not automatically grant every Fremen player cap 5.

**La La La removes free eligibility, not this paid method.** With Tleilaxu present, Fremen may submit a paid normal request within the current cap after free revival is prevented. Without Tleilaxu, the base purchase restriction still applies, leaving no ordinary return while free revival is prevented. This is composition of the corrected eligibility rule with La La La, not an explicit combined FAQ example.

**Keep Emperor-funded extras separate.** The recipient's ordinary purchase restriction should not block this independently authorized allied transaction. Its stated allowance and the official FAQ distinguish it from ordinary returns. No retrieved Fremen-specific worked example confirms the combination; this conclusion applies the general alliance text. Cancellation of that alliance ability must still prevent it.

These findings concern ordinary forces, not leader prices or independent card/special effects. A future modifier that increases valid free entitlement must not be defeated by hardcoding Fremen's lifetime normal allowance to 3; Recruits integration remains separately unsettled.

## Historical reproduction and retracted interpretation

Before the initial free-only change, `forceRevivalLimit` returned 5 for expanded Fremen and `forceRevivalQuote` priced its excess generically. The normal `revive` action checked only that ceiling and tank supply. A temporary internal fixture reproduced:

| State                                               | Observed pre-fix result                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fremen with Tleilaxu ceiling 5, no prior revival    | Normal request 5 returned all 5 and charged Fremen 4 spice.                                       |
| Fremen with settled `freeBlocked`, no prior revival | Normal request 3 returned 3 and charged Fremen 6 spice.                                           |
| Emperor allied to Fremen with settled `freeBlocked` | Separate extra request returned 3, charged Emperor 6, and left Fremen's ordinary usage unchanged. |

The original audit incorrectly labeled the first row a rules bug: **it is valid under FAQ p. 5**, assuming the stated Tleilaxu permission remains effective. The second fixture contained CHOAM but no Tleilaxu, so its paid ordinary returns still violate the base restriction. The third row establishes route separation. Fixtures used `applyAction` and passed response windows; no room/API actions were made. The successful baseline command was `node --import tsx /tmp/dune-fremen-revival-audit.mts`. This temporary script records behavior, but its console interpretation of the first row is obsolete and must not become a regression expectation.

The initial unconditional Fremen free-only bound in `forceRevivalRemaining` was based on this audit's incorrect conclusion and must not be retained for tables containing Tleilaxu. The corrected contract applies that free-only bound only when Tleilaxu is absent. Otherwise tank supply and the remaining applicable ordinary cap constrain the amount, with pricing handled separately. The independent runtime reviewer owns the correction and tests; this document does not certify the concurrent implementation.

Keep Emperor extras, Ghola and other special returns outside that ordinary helper. Regression coverage should allow the first historical purchase, reject the second, distinguish Tleilaxu presence from mere expansion selection, and check cap 3 versus permitted cap 5. Following a successful La La La interruption, the pending free request stops without charging or moving forces; Fremen with Tleilaxu may then make a new paid request. This correction does not decide earlier-paid Recruits accounting, refunds or pending-card timing.
