# Ordinary bribe controls

21 September 2026. A connected prototype exposes the existing authoritative
ordinary bribe action beneath the table. This does not complete negotiation,
Leader Skills or expansion acceptance.

## Payment and controls

Choose a non-allied recipient and a positive whole amount, then explicitly pay.
The payer loses spice immediately. The recipient's share is deferred until the
next Mentat Pause; it is not current spending money. The public chronicle records
the amount and recipient, while the panel exposes only its owning seat's supply
and incoming escrow. Self/allied targets, Mentat payments and Inflation Double
remain prohibited. Bribes are allowed out of turn at otherwise open boundaries.

These preserve the existing rules and [Bureaucrat source contract](BUREAUCRAT_PAYMENTS.md).
The official [November FAQ, p. 9](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=9)
places paid bribes in front of the recipient's shield until the next Mentat Pause.
Chat messages and verbal promises do not themselves transfer spice. Emperor
gifts to allies remain a separate power.

The server derives the owner's limits from existing uncommitted spice, excluding
sealed bids, paid support, Banker spending and other reserved costs. Existing
Truthtrance completion checks further constrain voluntary spending on isolated
copies; this projection does not commit actions or live continuations. The
target-specific Bureaucrat guard can cap unsupported combinations below five.
Supported payments use the existing saved redirect/allow decision. A pending
response, decision or forced continuation disables the payment controls.

The client retains an invalid amount or removed recipient as a disabled draft
rather than silently changing a payment. The explicit action uses existing
request/version handling: concurrent or stale submissions cannot debit twice.
After an uncertain result, reconcile the game before making another payment.
Only committed game state survives refresh; unsubmitted form drafts do not.

## Verification and remaining work

Focused tests cover out-of-turn transfer, JSON escrow/collection, private owner
budgets, target and integer validation, phase/response restrictions, auction
reservations, max/max-plus-one Truthtrance spending, Bureaucrat continuation and
rendered controls. Production HTTP checks exercise concurrent duplicate payment,
stale rejection, refresh and private views; existing Bureaucrat SQLite and all
four legal AI response tests remain applicable. Source-bound checks, browser
observations, preservation, independent review and Git delivery are retained
with the private checkpoint.

AI seats can receive and later spend bribes through ordinary resource handling;
existing Bureaucrat choices remain legal. Automated negotiations and strategic
bribe initiation wait for the post-feature AI stage. Textual deals are not
automatically enforced. Complete negotiation, responsive/assistive acceptance
and all legal expansion combinations remain open.
