## Basic prototype — 24 October 2026

**Prototyped / Partial.** The structured question asks whether the respondent
will make **one ground movement of at least N physical forces to a printed
territory before their current Shipment and Movement turn ends**. It concerns
future events after the answer, not earlier moves or sums of smaller groups.
Shipment, HMS relocation, free accompaniment and Ambassador relocation do not
count. A concealed No-Field marker itself is not a physical on-board force;
ordinary counters moving with it retain their actual count.

The general binding-while-possible/current-turn authority is the GF9 November
2020 FAQ printed p8, recorded in the [shipment source contract](TRUTHTRANCE_SHIPMENT_PROMISES.md#authority-and-supported-meaning).
Its worked example concerns shipment. Applying that rule to this existential
movement statement, the owned-action witness and accepted-order release policy
below are explicit software compositions, not a publisher movement-specific FAQ
or Basic adoption of the unofficial Advanced revision.

Basic tables with the declared native families can also ask in clean earlier
phases0–4 of the current round, with the owned preparations below. During
Movement, ask at a clean **active or still-unused
waiting turn in the current Shipment and Movement queue**, before or after the
active respondent's shipment, including selected E1/E2/E3 factions. No optional modules or Advanced
inherit this entry. The actual native roster, physical inventory and route

Later-round movement questions, compound movement/fact questions and wider

## Behavior and controls

- **Yes** stays pending across nonmatching moves while a legal owned completion
  remains. One matching physical commitment fulfills it permanently, before card
  disposal, Intrusion, Ambassador/Terror or other later arrival effects. It is
  not a promise to keep those forces alive afterward.
- **No** forbids every matching ordinary/card ground movement until the actual
  turn ends, including later Hajr and both Ornithopter groups. A first nonmatch
  or card-disposal pause does not fulfill it. Ending early cannot evade Yes.
- Feasible answers use actual board/typed groups and the shared human movement
  declaration validator, not the strategic bot shortlist. The finite search
  considers ordinary moves, held Hajr, both held/active Ornithopter modes and
  their original cohort, current city access, current native bonuses, held
  CHOAM Kulon, held Ghola force returns and the owned Box recovery described below. Preparations use
  private detached execution; actual cards retain
  their ordinary effects, counters, discard/response and arrival continuations.
  Unknown future draws/gifts and unresolved opposing outcomes are not invented.
- Voluntary moves/actions cannot remove the last legal completion. A declared
  native movement waits for its real counter response before fulfillment or
  impossibility. Opposing impossibility releases only the answers that cannot
  remain compatible: retain every No and still-feasible Yes in accepted order.
  An impossible large promise does not erase an independent smaller one.
- Authoring has dedicated movement labels, an exact public preview and a
  one-to-twenty input. Only the respondent sees feasible answers and the next
  owned completion action. Persistent promise/answer controls have no timer;
  busy controls submit nothing. Four existing profiles follow those same legal
  views without strategy changes or calibration.

This is bounded reachability, not complete arbitrary Truthtrance enforcement.
Other owned preparation families (including native relocation preparation),
later rounds, optional/Advanced profiles and comprehensive
combination/recovery/privacy/custody acceptance remain due.
