# Recruits and existing revival rules

Audit: 2026-09-06. Scope: the Recruits card interacting with prior revivals, existing unlimited/expanded allowances, prevention, Fremen ally grants and pending declarations. No runtime changes. This supplements [ECAZ_TREACHERY_RULES.md](./ECAZ_TREACHERY_RULES.md), which contains the original component figure and card-face provenance.

## Earlier-paid example that needs a concrete choice

Suppose Atreides has already revived 2 forces free and 1 for 2 spice, then Recruits is played while Revival remains open. The effective free rate becomes 4 and the ordinary cap 7. Three distinguishable policies are possible in software:

1. Preserve the payment; give 2 additional free revivals because only 2 actual free revivals were used.
2. Preserve the payment; give 1 additional free revival because all 3 prior returns consume the enlarged free allowance.
3. Refund the earlier payment under a retroactive reclassification, with resulting free usage recalculated.

The retrieved primary passages do not explicitly choose among these. These are alternatives to clarify, not recommended house rules. Current `freeRevivalRemaining` subtracts `p.revived`, which combines paid and free ordinary returns; simply multiplying its rate would silently select option 2. The stored total cannot reconstruct option 1 or 3 reliably without additional history. Likewise, silently restricting Recruits to the beginning of Revival would remove its printed during-Revival opportunity.

The clean source-supported case is play before any revival, or play after only free revivals with no unresolved declaration. This is a useful fixture boundary, not permission to represent the whole card as complete with a narrowed timing rule.

## Proposed software boundary

A pure helper should receive already-resolved facts, without deciding unsettled rules through parameter names:

- Is the card active for this turn?
- What is the current valid free rate after permissions/modifiers?
- Is taking free revival currently prohibited?
- What ordinary cap applies, and is a separate unlimited advantage still valid?
- What usage is being deducted, under an explicitly selected prior-paid policy?

Do not collapse prevention, free rate, ordinary limit, faction exceptions and earlier usage into one number. For example, free rate 0 is not equivalent to losing an unlimited paid allowance. Do not model La La La by permanently changing faction metadata.

Current integration points are `forceRevivalLimit`, `freeRevivalRemaining`, `forceRevivalQuote`, `beginRevival`, `finishRevival`, CHOAM/Tleilaxu response settlement, and `grantRevival`. Existing checks compare totals against hardcoded 3 to decide whether an advantage response is required. With an independent card cap, crossing 3 alone no longer proves that a player is exercising the Tleilaxu unlimited/expanded advantage. A pure quote helper alone cannot correct that response classification.

Pending transaction options also require an explicit policy: finish the already accepted request before allowing Recruits; permit withdrawal and a newly validated request; or allow the card to alter an uncommitted quote. Whichever boundary is chosen, persist the suspended request, actor, physical card ID, phase/turn, previous payment and exact continuation. Never consume Recruits and then reject its effect after mutating someone else's resources. Repeated requests and JSON reconnects must settle once.

## Verification and open decisions

This was a bounded source/document audit. No runtime test, helper edit, complete Recruits game, or direct combined-expansion publisher ruling is claimed. Findings were sent to `baliset_audit`, which owns the separate pure helper and tests.

`oxfmt --check docs/RECRUITS_RULES.md` validates document formatting only. Later behavior tests should name their interpretation and cover native unlimited factions, canceled versus independent limit sources, settled prevention, grant ordering, prior-free versus prior-paid usage, pending declarations and next-turn expiry. The remaining material decisions are **earlier-paid accounting/refunds**, **late-changing current rates**, and **pending-transaction timing**; direct confirmation would also strengthen the proposed independent-cap precedence and hypothetical unlimited/free 8 combination.
