## Source boundary and model contract

The [publisher CHOAM & Richese rulebook](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf), R2 pp6,10–11, was read in `/tmp/dune-rules/choam-lelekan-mirror.txt`, the verified publisher-authored mirror recorded in the existing source audit. Page6 establishes concealed shipment for one-force price, movement as forces, voluntary reveal before Battle, forced storm/worm reveal, one deployed marker and no consecutive reuse. Battle reveal occurs with the Battle Plan; ordinary Atreides dial inspection is prohibited in that battle. Allied shipment reveals immediately, after revealing any already deployed marker. Pages10–11 confirm one-force effects even for zero, Gamont reveal/return, zero-token leader/cards, reserve-constrained dial and allied payment/Heighliner details.

The intended `Player.noField?: RicheseNoField` stores the component separately. `p.forces` must continue to mean **actual units**, with reserves transferred only when the marker reveals. Concealed presence contributes one to occupancy and ordinary board-presence effects. It does not contribute a casualty unit, an extra reserve, or one guaranteed battle dial. The owner can privately derive prospective materialization as `min(token value, current reserves)`; a public count must not use this value.

Keep distinct helpers for actual unit count, effective presence, occupied location keys, and private prospective battle units. Replacing every `at()` or `fighterCount()` with one universal number would contaminate casualties, economic effects and secret projections. Existing pure reveal returns materialization facts, not force/Tanks mutations; the engine must apply those facts once with an event/version fence. Public marker projection must omit physical token IDs and hidden supply values that disclose denomination through elimination.

## Battle privacy and unresolved arithmetic

For a marker-only supported battle, maximum materialized ordinary units is bounded by denomination and current reserves, with zero dial available but mandatory leader rules unchanged. **Mixed ordinary forces plus marker is not resolved by this statement.** R2 says not to dial higher than reserves but does not answer whether this overrides existing forces, how mixed sectors combine, or how reserve commitments alter the limit. Do not silently implement `ordinary + min(value,reserves)` or `min(total,reserves)` and call it a verified FAQ formula. A narrow guard on unresolved mixed configurations is honest; the engine/UI must explain it without disclosing hidden value. Reserve changes after one plan seals require a stable commitment or explicit incompatible-action guard, not deferred creation of nonexistent units.

Ordinary `prescience` currently accepts `dial` in the server field list and `prescienceAnswer` validates it from actual forces. Remove that choice for an entitled target with a No-Field in this battle, enforce it on the server, and retain the prohibition in battle state after reveal removes the marker. Weapon/defense/leader information remains eligible under ordinary prescience. Update owner/allied beneficiary controls and bot selection; hiding a button alone is insufficient. Do not publish private prospective maxima or reasons to the opponent.

The special entire-plan power currently stores an ordinary plan and `viewGame.battle.fullPlanInsight` returns it in full to its owner before public reveal. The retrieved No-Field text does not explicitly reconcile entire-plan special Karama with dial secrecy. Do not send the dial through this property on an assumption, nor silently omit part of an advertised entire plan. Guard the unresolved intersection before spending Karama until the rule is settled. `Truthtrance` structured battle-plan claims, reachable-plan helpers, compliant-plan projections and historical truth answers are separate disclosure paths needing the same audit. The direct dial restriction does not automatically resolve every question about forces or future plans.

## Allied shipment and interruption contract

Allied shipment uses Richese's physical token but the ally's reserves and immediately becomes actual ally units. It must first reveal any existing own marker, and the next token cannot repeat the last own/allied use. A canceled shipment must not mutate last-use history or materialize a token merely to validate it. If the old-marker reveal and new allied shipment are separate irreversible steps, persist their stage so a retry cannot reveal twice or rollback a completed reveal accidentally.

The FAQ fixes the cost at one spice in a stronghold, two elsewhere, payable by either player to Guild or bank. Do not apply the ordinary amount-times-price helper to materialized three/five units. Heighliner triggers except when Guild is the shipped ally; the actor identity must therefore be the force recipient, not always the Richese power owner. The follow-up source audit now supplies a reviewed ordinary contract: recipient shipment allowance, private consent and typed physical-unit selection, either full payer or one each for a two-spice shipment. Fremen’s ordinary on-planet reserves are excluded. Only the Guild-rate/active-Karama precedence and additional unresolved entry combinations remain guarded here; see `RICHESE_ALLIED_SHIPMENT_AUDIT.md`. In particular, generic allied financial pledges are not a substitute for the printed either-payer choice.

Own hidden shipment has the same important actor/representation split: Guild income and BG accompaniment are shipment effects, but the logged amount and entry projection cannot reveal the token. Existing `commitShipment` can open both Guild response and BG decision before territory-entry effects; do not overwrite them with a new response. Existing unsupported simultaneous-entry handling should reject atomically rather than silently skip Terror/Ambassador effects. Canceling the No-Field power is not necessarily a canceled ordinary shipment: do not grant an automatic fallback shipment without a ruling.

## Map, UI, AI and additional effects

`components/game-table.tsx` directly enumerates forces for map stacks, movement sources, public rosters and Gamont controls, and its basic battle wheel uses `fighterCount`. A marker needs an explicit concealed glyph at its sector, private owner inspection, marker-specific selection, and a projected private planning bound. Do not inject fake `forces[key]=1` to make existing rendering work. That would spend/kill a nonexistent unit and mislead conservation tests. `game/bots.ts` likewise enumerates actual source groups and derives battle pools; update movement and planning with entitled projected options, while opponents see one uncertain presence rather than zero/three/five.

Audit generic `killTerritory`/Family Atomics/lasgun-shield, Guild cross-shipment or return-to-reserves, Hajr, Ornithopter, Smuggler, Moritani/Ecaz arrivals and force-transfer effects individually. The specific storm/worm/Gamont instructions do not prove that every generic “remove force” operation has the same reveal-and-return semantics. Mobile Stronghold and Homeworld containers must not be accepted just because a structural location passes. The existing pure module's ordinary Polar Sink address is valid; it does not authorize unsupported off-planet custody.
