## Evidence and status meanings

- **Counted**: a factory or data structure was inspected and its quantity was executed locally. This does not verify every printed name, rule, or face.
- **Partial**: executable behavior, an explanation, or a control exists; some rules, interactions, or presentation acceptance checks remain open.
- **Missing**: no inventory/implementation for that component family was found in the inspected source.
- **Unverified**: the audit has not established the claim against primary evidence or a relevant runtime check. This is different from proving an implementation absent.
- **Abstracted**: the digital game represents a physical component using state/controls rather than a card entity. Its behavior and information visibility still require verification.

The source hierarchy is printed GF9 components and official clarifications, followed by designer material. A mirrored publisher-authored PDF is identified as a mirror. Community inventories can help locate omissions but do not establish printed text or authorize tournament amendments.
