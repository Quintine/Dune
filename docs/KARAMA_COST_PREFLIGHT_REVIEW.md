## Finding

A pre-effect Karama discard cannot safely be persisted merely because `karamaSpendingBlock` accepted its card. Today the original public action remains atomic because `completeKarama`, all its continuations and voluntary promise checks execute on the same clone before it is returned. Several suffixes can reject after `discard`; others assume pending objects with non-null assertions and would fail or misapply a crossed save. A durable cost needs both a pure, direction-specific accepted-use validator and exact binding of the original opportunity. Do not run `completeKarama`, `finishResponse`, `resolveBattle` or the general normalizer as a speculative preflight: these can draw, shuffle, create UUIDs, alter phase, or produce successor discard frames.

The most concrete unsupported path is `finishResponse('richeseAuction',true)` at 9623: it always throws because canceled normal-auction counting remains unresolved. Reject this intended cancellation before spending either a printed card **or a BG Worthless card whose conversion would eventually execute it**. This is preservation of the existing implementation boundary, not a new rule answer.

### Two concrete transitive rejection families

`resumeChoamMovement` catches `validateMovementOrder` failure and abandons movement without rejecting cancellation. Preserve that accepted outcome. If the order is valid, its `completeMove` is outside that catch and can still reject on No-Field event/custody, flight selection, shipment commitments, or unsupported arrival. `finishMovedGroup` → `openTerritoryEntry` (5531) rejects competing Ambassador/Terror/intrusion parents; a final Ornithopter move runs that same arrival guard in its clone preflight at 5114. Extract a pure completed-order/arrival eligibility check over predicted resulting occupancy and flags, respecting which invalid orders become an accepted no-move. Do not merely call resumeChoamMovement on a clone: marker movement creates a UUID and other paths create frames.

`resolveBattle` reached through canceled Harkonnen allied traitor has explicit throws for unresolved Stone physical winner (8405), missing chosen Stone mode (8413), empty casualty options (8594), existing pending Auditor (8661), and existing Moritani retention (8695). Its discarded played cards additionally need strict physical custody and any cleanup-context prerequisites. The comparison/casualty functions are pure reusable checks; full resolution is not a safe preflight. Prior battle/Stone/late-defense validators cover parts of this, but do not mistake them for validating every resolution suffix.

## BG Worthless direction and nested response

`spendKarama` discards first; BG then creates `pendingKarama={owner,use}` plus `worthlessKarama` response. A later allowance calls `completeKarama` with the original use; a later cancellation leaves the underlying effect unperformed. Before accepting BG's initial cost, validate that the intended allowed use has an executable supported suffix. Then bind the original opportunity across conversion, not only the immediate conversion owner.

A printed card canceling an existing Worthless conversion is different: its canceled suffix restores the old response or payment selection. **Do not recursively reject it just because executing the saved old use would be unsupported.** A legacy BG attempt to cancel Richese auction counting can be safely canceled, because the old Richese response is restored without canceling Richese. Validate the pending use's shape, owner and bound older context, then the restoration branch, not its allowed-effect semantics.

For canceled auctionPayment conversion, `recoverAuctionPayment` (4880) either reopens payment or restarts the same unrevealed lot. Preserve the existing explicitly provisional recovery; do not choose a new rule. Bind bidder/lot/card and ensure its opener loop has an eligible seat in a malformed-save check. Do not blindly require current spice sufficient: insufficient funding is the very recovery branch. A second BG Worthless conversion is already prohibited while pendingKarama exists; printed cancellation of it remains valid.

## Minimum extractable validation architecture

Use a typed pure `validateKaramaUseContext(g,p,physicalCard,use,stage)` with stages that distinguish initial declaration, saved cost receipt, allowed BG conversion and canceling BG conversion. Return a canonical bound use/opportunity description, not a whole replacement Game. Reuse a second pure `validateCanceledResponseContext` exhaustive over the response union, with explicit pending-parent mapping above. Extract pure quoted Richese settlement, battle-resolution prerequisites and post-move arrival checks where required. Shared `suspendedControlsIntegrity` can validate restored controls, but its current loose generic response shape is not the exhaustive branch proof.

This is not a reason to disable all complex cancellations: retain existing accepted paths by checking their actual prerequisites and recording exact original context. An opportunity signature binds a checked state across save/reload; it does not replace the initial semantic checks. Never perform RNG simply to determine whether the suffix is executable.

Current generic discard validator only allows pendingRicheseGift/pendingIxAlly/pendingAmbassador with Box/Truth frames. New cost frames require narrow allowances for the genuine bound cancellation parents. Auditor lookup currently sees only live, Box/Truth and gift resumes (9052); include exact Karama target/nested-use contexts or a valid canceled Auditor frame will fail before retirement. Do not detach all pending records wholesale: retain their live semantic context and bind them.

Retire the Karama frame before calling its suffix. A suffix may create a new battleCleanup, battleResolved or Ornithopter discard frame. No caller, automatic loop or promise search may consume a second card, draw, or advance the restored controls while that successor frame remains pending. Preserve each sequence/event and producer boundary separately.

## Voluntary promises and simulation are additional pre-cost fences

The current public wrapper calls `reconcileBattlePromises(g,{actor,action})` and `reconcileShipmentPromises(g,{actor,action})` only after completeKarama/finalization (11485); these throw when voluntary spending makes that actor's promise impossible (8030,8092). `normalizeAutomaticGame` has no voluntary actor and instead may release an impossible promise. Therefore a saved cost cannot defer this validation until ordinary automatic recovery: that would convert an illegal voluntary spend into an automatic release.

Preflight needs the actor's post-cost **and intended-use** context, not just a removed-card context. In particular a shipping Karama can itself establish the rate that makes shipment feasible. Preserve that rate in a pure predicted context before checking the promise. Battle cancellation can change Voice or prescience; validate against the correct predicted constraints. Opponent consequences may legitimately release another player's promise. Where a response suffix is not yet represented by a safe pure prediction, retain its original atomic no-pause envelope until that proof is implemented rather than claiming a safely durable prefix. This does not require a new rules interpretation.

`findShipmentCompletion` calls inner dispatcher with internal `shipmentPreparation` for Ghola/Karama/escrow preparations (7962). The new cost producer must honor this internal path for deterministic **own shipping Karama only**: execute effect and physical removal on its private clone without a live cost frame, opportunity, RNG or general normalization. The current flag bypasses only ordinaryCardDiscard; it does not automatically cover a newly staged spendKarama. Do not allow a JSON field to select simulation. Keep battle's direct preparation helpers unchanged.

## Test contract for implementation

Use real declaration → predrain cost → JSON/recovery for all four uses and both card forms; verify exact card/owner/recipient/lot/pending binding, original card consumed once, effect once, and duplicate CAS/worker recovery. Add unsupported Richese count rejection before any spent card/version; nested printed cancellation of a saved BG count attempt must restore the original response. Exercise free auction acquisition with Ix decision and Harkonnen bonus, paid-sale cancellation without replaying bidder or seller payments, invalid Richese sold-lot/funding and cache-exhaustion suffixes, Moritani successor cleanup, Baliset cancellation restoring a final Ornithopter move, Harkonnen traitor battle resolution, and typed storm/worm parents.

At least one promise test must reject spending the actor's last preparation Karama for an unrelated cancellation and preserve state; another must accept shipping Karama that makes the promised shipment possible. Repeated private shipment search with Ghola+Karama+escrow must stay immutable and yield executable actions. Preflight repeated calls should be checked with RNG/UUID counters that remain zero. Frame projection must remain generic-only; do not derive a new public response window from hidden card holder identity or display pure settlement/private-promise evidence.

This audit identifies implementation prerequisites only. It does not settle canceled Richese auction counting, canceled BG payment recovery, Karama shipment card refund custody, Sapho/Guild ordering or Semuta full-hand ordering.
