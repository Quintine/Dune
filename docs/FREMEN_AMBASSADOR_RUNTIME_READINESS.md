# Fremen Ambassador runtime readiness

Historical design review. The implemented contract and final validation supersede the proposals below: [FREMEN_AMBASSADOR_IMPLEMENTATION.md](FREMEN_AMBASSADOR_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

Read-only implementation audit, 7 September 2026. The smallest useful addition is a beneficiary-owned remote board-group selection under the existing Ambassador event, followed by a bounded arrival continuation. Existing typed force and marker operations are reusable. Ordinary movement completion is not: it spends a movement and shipment opportunity. A general recursive Ambassador stack or a new durable discard framework is unnecessary for this effect.

This is a design review of the current source, not a released implementation or full expansion certification. Runtime line pointers below describe the reviewed snapshot and may move during integration. Source interpretation is coordinated with the independent publisher-source reviewer; the earlier [remaining-effects readiness](ECAZ_REMAINING_EFFECTS_READINESS.md) is historical where it leaves remote relocation and recursive Ambassador entry unresolved.

## Rules composition and current gates

The [GF9 Ecaz & Moritani rulebook, pp.7–8](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf) assigns the effect to Ecaz or its ally and relocates one group already on the board to a territory, retaining storm and occupancy restrictions. The source reviewer establishes a direct remote relocation, parallel to ordinary worm transportation: no ordinary adjacency, distance allowance, or clear path through intermediate territories. Neither reserves nor Homeworlds are source/destination territories; the latter exclusion is explicit in E3 p.10. Discovery locations that are territories require board support currently absent from this implementation.

One group may select partial counts from several sectors of one territory. Selected source sectors and the destination must be clear of storm. Elites remain typed forces. A concealed No-Field moves under its existing movement rules, counts as one effective presence even when its hidden value is zero, and does not reveal merely by relocating. The Hidden Mobile Stronghold is a separate destination: the [Ixians & Tleilaxu rules, p.9](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf) restrict non-Ixian entry to its pointing territory. Relocating forces never moves the stronghold itself or collects spice along its route.

Same-territory movement to a different sector remains possible under the November FAQ movement clarification; require actual relocation and do not manufacture another territory-entry reaction. In phase5, existing Baliset restrictions apply because this is movement into CHOAM occupation, not shipment; phase1 relocation does not meet Baliset’s phase condition. Reuse `balisetPrevents` for the existing restriction. Opening a new reactive CHOAM movement decision requires this effect’s own return link rather than `pendingChoamMove`, whose validator and completion spend ordinary movement.

If no legal relocation exists after commitment, follow an explicitly documented unavailable-effect interpretation: token committed, no group moved, finish once. Do not add an arbitrary beneficiary decline to an otherwise mandatory effect. Recompute current legal availability without leaking hidden No-Field values.

Two useful consequences bound the implementation:

- The original entrant cannot be Ecaz or its ally. Therefore a legal beneficiary is not that entrant, and their relocation cannot trigger a second Ecaz Ambassador while the same alliance remains in force. `canTriggerAmbassador` already expresses this exclusion. BG copying the Fremen effect changes neither beneficiary entitlement nor the original physical-token provenance.
- An active phase-5 Ornithopter belongs to the original entrant. It cannot belong to this effect's beneficiary. Preserve the original flight, its original-unmoved cohort, counters, card custody and remaining selection unchanged; do not create another flight or subtract the relocated beneficiary's forces from that cohort.

`allowedEntry` currently bans allied co-occupation outside Polar Sink. That is not complete Ecaz legality: E3 p.8 Occupy permits Ecaz and its ally to co-occupy and treats them as one faction. Its downstream combat/collection/victory implementation remains separately incomplete. Reusing that blanket rejection must remain an explicit development gate, not a claimed printed Fremen Ambassador restriction. The same applies to the existing simultaneous Terror/BG arrival gate and absent Discovery/Homeworld modules. Full Advanced/expansion starts are already disabled independently; those start gates do not prevent implementing and testing this bounded effect in conserved development positions.

## Bounded state and arrival continuation

Proposed contract; names are implementation choices:

1. Keep the real entry producer, owner offer and physical token commitment in `decideAmbassador`. A triggered Fremen effect, including BG copying, opens an event-bound `move` stage for the selected beneficiary. Keep the original entrant, original entry territory/sector, turn/phase, physical token, copy provenance and `resume` untouched. Do not open a second confirmation after the beneficiary has submitted a valid move.
2. A pure selection quote validates current event/beneficiary, original used/removed token provenance, one board source group, selected ordinary/elite/marker custody, storm endpoints, destination stance, mobile destination rule and applicable occupancy/Baliset restrictions. Permit a genuine different-sector move within the source territory, with no token-entry reaction. It returns the exact relocation plus arrival requirements. It must not use random values, expose marker value, spend resources, set `active`, or demand the beneficiary's ordinary movement allowance.
3. Commit remove/place/stance/marker event and one relocation log once. Keep `shipped`, `moved`, `hajr`, `karamaShipping`, movement queue, Guild timing, Sapho ordering and original flight unchanged. Freeze a small completed relocation receipt while child reactions remain; recovery must resume the suffix rather than select or move the group again.
4. A clean destination calls `finishAmbassador` once. Reactive CHOAM Baliset, if offered for this phase5 relocation, needs a source-tagged pending order and return link that preserves this independent movement; do not reuse `resumeChoamMovement` unchanged. For an actual Terror or BG reaction, keep the Ambassador in a non-actionable arrival stage and attach an explicit return link to its event. A small typed child continuation is sufficient; no second Ambassador is opened for Ecaz/its ally. Only after the child finishes may `finishAmbassador` replenish a completed five-token cohort and restore the original entrant or worm queue.
5. If both BG intrusion and Terror apply, either implement a source-justified local ordering or retain a clearly named unsupported simultaneous-arrival gate before relocation. Existing code opens intrusion first and rejects a competing token; that is a gate, not an authoritative ordering. Source priority must not be invented by the new helper.

A nested Terror alliance can legitimately end Ecaz's alliance with the beneficiary. That does not invalidate the already committed relocation or entitle a replay. ## Concrete reactive Baliset adapter

Root's planned contract fits the existing engine without inventing an ordinary move: `MovementOrder` gains `source?: 'ambassador'` and `ambassadorEvent?: string`; `pendingChoamMove` owns that exact order only while CHOAM decides or its Worthless response is pending. The original Ambassador entry remains live and bound to the source event. Offer this reaction only in phase5, for another faction moving into a different territory currently occupied by CHOAM; neither within-territory sector transfer nor phase1 relocation opens it.

Do not route this order through `pendingFremenMovement`, `pendingIxMove`, `movementOrderRange`, `validateFlightSelection` or `finishOrnithopter`: those are faction/ordinary-move opportunities, not a free Ambassador relocation. Existing Ornithopter-discard integrity forbids a live pending CHOAM order; the actual producer drains its original retirement before opening the Ambassador, so that exclusion remains correct.

Minimum Baliset evidence is a genuine entry followed by a remote declared move into CHOAM, then each of: decline; successful Baliset and a different legal destination; printed cancellation; BG cancellation allowed; BG cancellation itself canceled; unavailable declared card and source revalidation. Repeat final allowance through SQL CAS and check exactly one relocation, marker-event rotation and eventual cohort finish. Mutating only the saved Ambassador event or copied-source binding must reject before Karama cost and on a paid final allowance. Parent loss must never be converted into a silent ordinary movement fizzle.

## Privacy, automation and focused evidence

Use the existing owner offer and beneficiary choice projections. Return available physical counts, own elite counts and the beneficiary's own marker selection only to that beneficiary. Other seats need the public effect/stage/beneficiary and committed visible board movement, never marker value, private hand/traitor identities or raw saved controls. Pure option generation must not allocate marker/event IDs or advance random state. An active entry decision must keep the original entrant from moving while the beneficiary or child owner acts.

A compact verification set should include:

- Actual enemy move or shipment into a placed Fremen token, both Ecaz-self and allied beneficiary, with remote nonadjacent multi-sector mixed ordinary/elite move. Test BG copy independently; preserve original entry costs, counts and active player.
- Storm source/destination, invalid sector, typed over-selection, malformed/stale parent and marker events, unsupported Occupy/combined-arrival boundaries, mobile pointing territory and unplaced mobile marker. Illegal actions must leave input and persisted SQL state unchanged.
- No-Field alone and accompanied physical forces, including hidden zero; only one marker-event rotation and no reveal/reserve loss. Compare non-owner projections under hidden-value perturbation.
- Clean finish, Terror decline, Sabotage/Robbery discard suffix, intrusion decline/allow/cancel and permitted BG conversion. Verify original source/counters and exactly one cohort renewal after the final child, including a fifth-token BG copy.
- Original first-two-group Ornithopter arrival resumes the unchanged second-group cohort; original last-group card is already discarded exactly once. The beneficiary's independent move never increments either flight or ordinary counters.
- Actual worm-origin entry with a non-Fremen beneficiary, child reaction and remaining worm queue; summoned-worm interruption if permitted by existing controls. Reconnect, JSON round-trip and competing final child requests must move once, finish once, and preserve private projections.
- All four AI profiles select only projected legal options, and the UI offers a readable typed group/destination editor rather than a generic card-selection tail. No unrelated full-game calibration is required to establish this bounded slice.
