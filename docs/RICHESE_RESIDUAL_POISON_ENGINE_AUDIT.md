# Residual Poison: source and engine contract

Audit: 2026-09-06. The source findings below informed the bounded runtime integration recorded at the end. Historical hook and proposed-contract sections describe the pre-integration audit; they do not supersede the final checkpoint. Complete expansion compliance remains unverified.

## Evidence and confirmed effect

Photograph SHA256: `9c1d3e65fa33489e7675055ed1fc932fefcb79f6053ee365ddee6737ae10b81f`.

The [GF9 CHOAM/Richese rulebook](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf), pp5–6, supplies the ten-card inventory and normal discard routing. The readable publisher-authored local mirror is `/tmp/dune-rules/choam-lelekan-mirror.pdf` and `.txt`; provenance is recorded in `RICHESE_ACQUISITION_RULES.md`. Direct publisher PDF requests again returned 403. Bounded publisher-indexed searches for Residual Poison and its leader wording found no card-specific FAQ, priority exception or exceptional-leader pool ruling.

The effect targets the actual opposing combatant, not any chosen faction or an ally's opponent. It is a Special Treachery Card, not a poison weapon in a sealed battle plan. It has no activation payment, recipient consent, ordinary Karama cancellation, automatic traitor call, battle win, force loss, Face Dance, technology transfer or battle-income event. Discard the physical card once through normal routing; it does not return to the Richese cache. No Spice Bank, killer, Emperor or Tleilaxu income is generated by this death. Zoal needs no strength calculation because the card expressly produces no death bounty.

## Leader pool: composition versus unresolved custody

Use current control and current battle availability, not `target.leaders` alone. The [base rules](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/Dune-Rulebook.pdf), pp10–11 and17, restrict leader use to one territory per turn, permit reuse in that territory, describe captive battle use and return, and exclude the Kwisatz Haderach from ordinary leader deaths. Thus the ordinary compositional pool is living controlled leader discs available in this territory; exclude leaders already used elsewhere, dead discs, native leaders currently controlled by someone else, Cheap Hero cards and the Kwisatz Haderach. Voice constraints on Treachery types do not make a physical leader unavailable.

The [Ixian/Tleilaxu rules](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf), p7, expressly add revived foreign leaders to the Tleilaxu leader pool. Consequently a living controlled foreign ghola belongs in the ordinary available-leader composition, rather than acquiring immunity because its native faction differs. Zoal is one physical eligible disc, with the same random weight as another eligible disc. This is general-rule composition, not a retrieved Residual-specific answer.

The [Ecaz/Moritani clarification](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf), p9, distinguishes Duke Vidal's controller, one-battle tenure and Ecaz-only revival. A living currently controlled, battle-available Duke is not given poison immunity. Use his canonical separate physical disc; death must preserve his history in the Tanks and must not generate a fresh native disc, return him alive to supply, or revive him for the current controller. Existing unresolved advanced Harkonnen/Duke configurations remain implementation guards. Do not pretend those guards are printed immunity.

For ordinary available captives, the general battle-use rule supports pool inclusion. Technical death cleanup must clear the dead captive's temporary captivity correctly, preserve its native revival identity and apply immediate captive return if Harkonnen loses its last native leader. The base rules explicitly describe those return/death rules, but the retrieved material does not specify public disclosure of an unrevealed captive killed by this card. Current concealed-capture projection intentionally shows stale public leader status. Logging the victim's name unconditionally would defeat that policy. This exceptional visibility question merits an explicit unresolved guard or a verified disclosure policy; do not silently exclude only the secret captive from a supposedly uniform random pool. Likewise, nested captured-foreign-ghola or captured-Duke lifecycle cannot be inferred by copying a normal death counter update. Normal foreign-ghola control and the currently supported uncaptured Duke case are technical implementation work, not inherently source ambiguities.

## Timing and a usable multiplayer opportunity

The existing card guide narrows the face to before an opponent chooses a leader. The actual plural/general wording does not expressly authorize playing after the holder has committed its own leader. The conservative supported timing is before either combatant has committed a leader. Any complete sealed plan has chosen a leader, even if its identity is hidden. A leader-valued Prescience answer also chooses one; a nonleader answer does not itself do so. Do not inspect the hidden identity to decide whether timing is open.

Base p23 explicitly orders Voice before Prescience and allows Truthtrance throughout that interaction. It does not assign Residual Poison a position relative to those powers. The card supplies no aggressor-first privilege, turn-order rule for simultaneous plays, or authority to invalidate an already selected leader. A fixed Residual-before-Voice or Residual-after-Voice checkpoint would be a scheduling convention, not a verified special FAQ ruling.

Simply adding an anytime button before the first sealed plan is insufficient: the opponent or an AI can seal first before the holder receives a usable opportunity. Creating a public decision only when somebody holds Residual reveals possession. Automatically passing only the nonholders leaks the same information through progression.

A privacy-preserving implementation option is an identically projected **universal pre-leader opportunity for both combatants**, with genuine ready/pass decisions from both and no automatic nonholder passes. Its purpose is to relinquish an optional card opportunity, not confirm automatic bookkeeping. Keep the labels and public progression independent of possession. It must finish before leader-valued Prescience answers and full-plan sealing can close the opportunity. Preserve Voice-before-Prescience and active Truthtrance answering. A fixed ordering for this checkpoint still needs to be identified as application scheduling unless further authority resolves it; alternatively integrate readiness with preparation actions without inventing a faction-specific priority. Do not claim that a timeout alone supplies the same right. A later acquisition/revival reopening a previously relinquished opportunity is not answered by the retrieved card text.

## Current engine hooks and concrete risks

- `chooseBattle`, `battlePreparation`, `finishBattlePreparation`, `combatResponses`: establish the existing funding/power/Voice/Prescience/full-plan sequence. A new opportunity must persist through these continuations without replacing another owner's decision. `Battle.prepared` alone is not a sufficiently precise pre-leader boundary.
- `controlledLeaders` plus the availability predicate in `validatePlan`: suitable starting point for controlled physical discs, including the separate Duke. Do not use `captureCandidates` merely because its filter looks similar. Do not copy Terror Assassination's native-five-only pool or bounty: its text and support guards are different.
- `battlePlan`, `prescienceAnswer`, advanced `fullPlan`: close the opportunity at the correct public commitment boundary. Preserve target-first full-plan sealing and never reveal a selected leader merely to explain a disabled card. A previously answered nonleader Prescience constraint can become infeasible after a death; add explicit feasibility handling, not an unreachable mandatory plan.
- `reconcileBattlePromises`: opposing involuntary death can release an impossible promise; it must not reject a legal randomized hit and redraw until a promised leader survives. Unaffected promises remain binding. The holder's voluntary action must still respect its own existing commitments. Validate all nonrandom facts before drawing; do not consume RNG in private availability projection.
- `resolveBattle` currently owns a local death helper and later captive/Duke cleanup. Extract or call a dedicated non-battle leader-death operation rather than invoking battle resolution. Increment exactly one death, clear obsolete `usedAt`, repair custody, and then reconcile feasibility. Do not consume a living Duke merely because the card was used in his prospective battle.
- `projectLeader` conceals captured state from unauthorized viewers. A public victim field, random-pool count, rejection reason or before/after projection may pierce that concealment. Project only authorized information and guard unsupported secret-captive disclosure uniformly, not by looking at a hidden selected plan.
- `pendingNullentropy` and `resolveTruthAction` must retain their early lock/answer priority. Source-card commitments must reuse `transferCardBlock` for held gifts, saved accepted Ix replacement, Harkonnen return requirements and battle/Truthtrance commitments. The card can settle synchronously, so it needs no new cancellable response or long-lived played-card escrow. It must not overwrite a paused gift, auction or search simply because it is categorized as Special.

## Proposed action/state/projection contract

Bind the preparation opportunity to a server-generated battle event, not merely territory and turn, since several battles can occur in one territory.

```ts
// Scheduling choice must be documented separately from card rules.
Battle.preLeader?: {
  event: string;
  ready: string[]; // actual combatant declarations, never inferred from hand
};

{ type: 'card', card: canonicalResidualId,
  target: actualOpponentId, event: battleOpportunityEvent }
{ type: 'battlePreparationReady', event: battleOpportunityEvent }

// Entitled holder only; no opponent pool, leader choices or eligibility metadata.
residualPoison: null | { card: string; event: string | null; blocked: string | null };
```

Validate exact held card identity, actor/target, event, phase, pre-leader timing, source commitments, supported custody and nonempty actual pool before any mutation or RNG. The server selects one uniformly from physical eligible IDs, applies one no-bounty death, discards once, performs custody/promise reconciliation and preserves the battle and remaining preparation state. The request never chooses the victim or supplies the random index. A singleton pool settles without an acknowledgment. Resolve genuine choices only; do not add a victim-confirm dialog.

Keep random resolution inside the authoritative action clone and normal room CAS. Concurrent stale submissions may calculate private trial outcomes but only one commit is exposed. Retrying a committed version cannot kill another leader. Persist only the settled state/public-safe event, not an unredacted pool snapshot that leaks through views. Legacy battles already holding sealed plans must not reopen the timing window.

## Focused acceptance checklist

1. Ordinary participant/opponent, exact canonical card, correct event; outsider, same-faction nonparticipant, empty pool and stale/sealed use reject without card/death/RNG mutation.
2. Available same-territory used leader included; dead and elsewhere-used excluded; uniform eligible-disc selection; Cheap Hero and Kwisatz never selected.
3. Foreign ghola, ordinary captured leader, last-native Harkonnen death and supported Duke physical custody; explicit guard for unresolved concealed-captive disclosure/nested custody. Zoal death gives zero spice regardless of opponent leader.
4. Exactly one normal discard, no cache return, bounty, payment, force loss, battle win, capture, Face Dance or tech transfer; all physical leader/card histories conserved.
5. Guaranteed opportunity against fast human/AI sealing; identical nonholder/holder public checkpoint shape; no auto-pass possession oracle. Leader Prescience/full-plan commitments close timing without leaking identity.
6. Voice, nonleader Prescience, genuine Truthtrance overlay and an involuntarily impossible promise; keep unaffected constraints and finishable planning. No random validation or conditional redraw.
7. Reserved gift/accepted Ix/Harkonnen-return/paid Box cases, explicit parent preservation and no silent decision replacement.
8. JSON restoration, owner/outsider projection equality where secrets differ, authentic concurrent CAS, duplicate request/reconnect, four AI profiles and no extra confirmation for the resolved death.

No extra user question was sent. Guaranteed opportunity scheduling and exceptional secret-captive disclosure are the material outstanding boundaries; ordinary no-bounty death, controlled available-leader filtering, disposal and continuation are implementable technical work.
