# Richese auction rules and integration contract

Audit: 2026-09-06. This document covers the complete core bidding pipeline: the compulsory cache lot, both alternative methods, advanced Black Market using all three methods, associated acquisition/payment effects, and the separate special Karama purchase. It is a source audit and implementation contract, not certification that Richese or a combined expansion mode is playable. Existing implementation-status gates remain in force.

## Printed rules, by checkpoint

The source anchor is R2 pp.4–6,10–12: ten cache cards stay outside the hand; one revealed cache lot replaces a normal lot and is declared first or last before Ixian preparation. It uses Once Around or Silent. Other buyers pay Richese; Richese pays Emperor/bank. Discards enter normal discard. Ordinary Karama acquisition is barred. Once Around gives each other faction one ascending bid/pass in a chosen direction, then Richese's final opportunity. Silent reveals eligible bidders' simultaneous offers, including zero, with storm-order ties. A zero result permits cache keep/remove. Advanced Black Market offers one concealed hand card before Declaration, permits all three methods, allows Atreides inspection and unverified sales claims, pays Richese, and shrinks the normal pool only after a sale; no bid means retain. Direct-cache purchases cannot use Ixian allied replacement; Black Market purchases can, even for Richese-family cards. Harkonnen bonus applies to both. Juice of Sapho works in Once Around. Cancellation prevents cache auction at round start or Black Market sale. The special power buys a privately chosen cache card for three spice. [R2](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf)

Ordinary bidding starts at the eligible storm-order opener, requires positive opening bids and increases, and rotates the next lot's opener from the prior opener rather than the winner. All-full hands cannot bid. Allied funding permits a bid larger than the bidder's own spice; the number of held cards is public during bidding, while spice holdings remain private. [B19 pp.8,12](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/Dune-Rulebook.pdf)

For Black Market with ordinary bidding, preserve the ordinary opener progression into subsequent ordinary lots. Alternative methods return normal bidding to storm order. The once-around traversal uses physical seated-circle order, not `g.order` (which begins from the storm); reversing direction must also work with unoccupied circles. Silent ties use `g.order`. A Juice of Sapho modification needs its own validated final-order transformation, not an unrelated reset of the ordinary opener. The component audit read its physical face: it permits intervention during the applicable phase and does not impose a before-first-bid restriction. Together with the Once Around FAQ, moving an eligible unacted bidder after Richese is supported composition; it does not authorize repeating a completed bid. The pure helper exposes `moveRicheseBidderLast` for that order change, while the engine must validate the actual card and its timing.

## Explicit source boundaries

These questions cannot be silently represented as settled FAQ compliance:

1. **Canceled compulsory lot and an exhausted cache.** The start-of-round reduction and the permission to prevent the cache auction are both printed, but the cancellation table does not say whether a replacement ordinary lot is restored. Nor does it prescribe exhausted-cache or zero-eligible-player arithmetic. Preserve separate count facts so an eventual ruling changes one policy rather than corrupting custody. Never draw a negative count.
2. **Black Market count wording.** Its specific rule conditions reduction on a sale. The embedded Ixian answer refers more broadly to cards put up for auction. The more specific sale condition supports no reduction for an unsold/canceled Black Market offer. Record that composition explicitly; do not decrement merely on declaration.
3. **Self-purchase of a Black Market card.** The generic alternative methods include Richese, but Black Market says Richese receives all payments and already owns the card. No retrieved paragraph specifies seller exclusion, a self-payment loop, an Emperor payment, or whether self-retention is a sale reducing the normal pool. Do not borrow the cache self-payment exception without a ruling.
4. **Ixian advanced Technology on special lots.** I20 p.9 broadly applies before bidding on a card and before Atreides inspection. R2's explicit restriction concerns the _ally's post-purchase replacement_, not Technology. Consequently that FAQ cannot justify disabling Technology on special lots. No retrieved Richese paragraph explains an exchanged cache lot's new public face, or the custody of a substituted Black Market lot if nobody bids. This is a genuine interaction to resolve before certifying the combined implementation.
5. **Economic interruptions during Silent.** Printed simultaneous physical offers do not specify asynchronous network reservation or what to do when a later effect changes funds. Internal reservations are necessary to reproduce simultaneous commitment, but a policy allowing players to revise already revealed information or silently reducing bids would be an extra rule. Use immutable hidden submissions and prevent double-spending without exposing reserved amounts.
6. **Separate Richese income cancellation.** The R2 table specifies preventing the sale/auction. It does not expressly create an additional post-sale cancel-income window. Reusing Emperor's income response for a sale paid to Richese would invent that window and potentially misroute money. General Karama composition may require further review; do not assume two independent chances from one listed power.
7. **Mandatory last lot after a normal all-pass ending.** Mandatory cache auction plus its last-position FAQ supports running it after the ordinary pool ends. This is a defensible composition, not a directly answered all-pass example. Store this boundary explicitly and test it.

Technology is once per bidding round, occurs before the next card's bidding/Atreides inspection, and swaps an exact hand card for the offered card. The Ixian ally ability is a different post-purchase discard/draw transaction. [I20 pp.8–9](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf)

## Concrete engine contract

This section records the original software proposal. The implemented checkpoint below supersedes its proposed field names where different; it does not resolve the listed source ambiguities.

Use a persisted phase wrapper separate from the existing normal `Game.auction`. Suggested checkpoints are `blackMarketOffer`, `blackMarketLot`, `declaration`, `cachePosition`, `cacheFirst`, `normalPreparation`, `normalLots`, `cacheLast`, and `complete`. Store declaration eligibility/count, each justified pool reduction, cache position, Black Market outcome and normal opener cursor. A checkpoint is consumed once; JSON reload must not repeat RNG, collection, pool preparation or sale effects.

`Game.richeseAuction` should discriminate `origin: 'cache' | 'blackMarket'` and `method: 'normal' | 'onceAround' | 'silent'`. Store seller, lot ID, exact physical card ID, face visibility, pending declaration/cancellation, eligible participants, order/cursor or sealed offers, winning bid/funding, zero disposition, and a typed resume checkpoint. Physical identity and transaction origin must remain separate. The wrapper owns the pending lot; no second mutable copy of its card can appear in hand, cache or ordinary pool. A reserved hand card must still count against hand capacity until the sale transfers custody.

For ordinary-method Black Market, adapt existing ascending bid validation without letting ordinary all-pass cleanup touch a seller's card. Once Around needs one immutable turn per eligible participant plus Richese's final opportunity; an ordinary repeated-bid loop is incorrect. Silent needs a server-private submission map with each submitter's amount and authorized funding split. Public projection shows submitted status only until atomic revelation; the submitter alone sees their offer. Do not expose other players' maximum bids or pledge balances. Capture the applicable tie order for the lot.

Introduce a sale adapter rather than constructing a fake ordinary auction:

- `currentSale`: winner, card, amount, declared funding, origin, recipient policy, Ix-ally eligibility, and typed continuation.
- Cache purchase by another player routes money to seller; self-purchase uses the ordinary Emperor/bank rule. Black Market uses its own recipient policy, with the self-purchase ambiguity isolated.
- Payment occurs once before existing post-purchase decisions. Ixian ally replacement applies by sale origin, then the applicable income handling and Harkonnen bonus continue once. Do not award bonuses on an unsold retained card.
- Preserve current ordinary Emperor self-buy behavior, cancellation, Harkonnen capacity checks and no-choice automatic completion. A Richese lot must never reach ordinary Karama free-purchase, auction-payment conversion or Karama-backed unaffordable bidding paths.
- Cancellation stores no untrusted arbitrary action. Revalidate the intended card and continuation, preserve concealed identities, and let the shared automatic-response machinery close windows with no eligible cancellation choice.

Audit cross-cutting spend paths: `pledgeAid`, `uncommittedSpice`, `payWithAlly`, `karamaSpendingBlock`, CHOAM cash-in, Harkonnen exchanges, special purchases, allied gifts and Truthtrance promises currently assume ordinary auction/battle commitments. Adding only a second auction object will otherwise allow a reserved bid's spice or a seller's card to be spent elsewhere.

Current relevant functions are `setAuction`, `offerAuctionTechnology`, `offerAuctionPeek`, `auctionNext`, `settleAuction`, `continueAuctionSale`, `auctionBonus`, `nextAuction`, `recoverAuctionPayment`, and the `pendingIxAlly` response/decision paths in `game/engine.ts`. Their direct `g.auction!` assumptions need the sale adapter or an explicit ordinary-only guard. `nextPhase` must be reached only after the wrapper has consumed the last required checkpoint.

## Required evidence before enabling the faction

Test both cache positions and both cache methods; all three Black Market methods; changing/full hand eligibility; all-zero, exhausted/short ordinary deck and ordinary early termination; both direction choices with sparse circles; Silent equal bids, zero bids and simultaneous submissions; own/allied payment conservation; exact custody across Karama cancellation and restart; private last-lot/Black Market/sealed-offer projections; Harkonnen and Ixian origin distinctions; all four AI profiles using only their views; duplicate submissions and stale CAS at each suspended checkpoint. Explicitly test special purchases and gifts against reserved cards. Any unresolved combined-rule behavior stays identified and gated, rather than being described as complete rules compliance.

The source audit itself changed no runtime code or availability gates. Card custody, phase integration, payment and unresolved source policies remain the engine integration owner’s responsibility. No live room or availability gate was changed by this subtask.

## Initial integrated development checkpoint — 6 September 2026 (superseded by final verification below)

The current wrapper is `Game.richeseBidding`, with `blackMarketOffer`, `declaration`, `cacheOffer`, `lot`, `normal` and `complete` stages. Event IDs distinguish owner decisions and auction submissions. `Game.richeseAuction` carries the separate lot; `richeseFunding` tracks its own/ally commitments, and `currentAuctionSale` preserves origin and continuation through the existing post-sale windows. No selectable mode gate was opened.

Implemented within the reviewed boundary:

- Basic cache first/last declaration and both alternative methods; normal-pool preparation/continuation, including reaching the last cache lot after ordinary bidding finishes. Cache identity remains owner-private before its offer.
- Advanced Black Market normal, Once Around and Silent offers; other-buyer sales; zero retention and cancellation without a fictitious sale reduction. Seller-positive bids are rejected pending the self-purchase ruling. Public claims are distinct from the hidden face.
- Exact own/authorized ally payment, reserved funding, eligibility and event validation; private sealed submissions followed by joint revelation. Cache self-payment routes to Emperor/bank; other-buyer proceeds route to Richese.
- Atreides Black Market inspection and applicable income/Harkonnen continuation; Ixian ally replacement follows transaction origin rather than the printed card family. Advanced Ixian Technology on special lots is separately guarded pending replacement-custody interpretation.
- Owner-only declaration/offer/disposition controls and entitled card inspectors, plus funded bid/pass or zero submission. Existing interruption/decision UI priority is preserved.
- Four AI profiles use their own projections for cache choices and all offered bidding methods. They currently decline optional Black Market at the prelude, and never submit a positive seller Black Market bid. This is a bounded policy, not a complete strategic evaluation.

Still blocked or incomplete: compulsory-cache cancellation count, exhausted-cache arithmetic, positive Black Market self-purchase, special-lot Technology custody, all ordinary Richese card-effect actions, actual Juice of Sapho play, and the full combined expansion rules. The pure Sapho reorder helper does not expose a playable card action. Special Karama acquisition and allied card gifting are not certified by this auction checkpoint.

### Runtime inheritance and reservation clarification

The physical Richese Karama has `effect: 'karama'` and inherits the existing generic handler if acquired in a development fixture. Earlier statements that all ten effects were wholly disabled should be read as the original inventory checkpoint, not the current runtime state. Remaining card-effect integration and full interaction verification are incomplete; actual Juice of Sapho card play remains inert and live expansion starts stay gated.

The engine now rejects playing a Black Market card reserved for sale and prevents a donor reducing pledged funding during unfinished Silent bidding. The latter avoids probing private sealed commitments through success/error limits. Independent review, recovery and browser verification remain pending until their checks finish. Internal rule navigation now tracks query changes and controlled topic-open state, preserving the destination after filter resets; browser confirmation of that fix is pending.
