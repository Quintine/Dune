## Exact returning group

Southern applies to actual starred counters revived by an otherwise supported producer: ordinary revival, Ghola, or an eligible Ambassador effect. It excludes ordinary counters, leaders, preexisting reserves, Face Dancer replacement and returning surviving forces. Preserve the physical starred identity in Basic too. Advanced permits only one Fedaykin revival per turn across producers; Basic does not acquire that Advanced limit. [GF9 E3, p.16](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf#page=16), [November FAQ, p.2](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=2).

Tleilax names the ordinary Free Revival source. A paid counter in the same request is excluded. Modifiers to that ordinary free allowance retain its source. E1 distinguishes free revival from Ghola; zero spice alone therefore cannot classify a Ghola or Ambassador return as Free Revival for deployment. This distinction composes the printed sources; no retrieved FAQ directly answers this Homeworld/Ghola interaction. [GF9 E1, p.7](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf#page=7). Keep any unresolved Ambassador quota restrictions from its [existing audit](TLEILAXU_AMBASSADOR_RULES.md).

## Population and immediate timing

The base return goes into reserves. E3 maintains high benefits while the minimum native population is present and inactivates them below it. Its nearby immediate-qualification language concerns occupation, not an explicit revival-trigger ordering. [Base, p.9](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/Dune-Rulebook.pdf#page=9), [E3, p.9](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf#page=9).

Ordinary free-rate pricing remains the pre-return quotation described in [benefit rules](HOMEWORLD_BENEFITS_RULES.md). Eligibility for a newly triggered deployment is a separate question. Depositing the complete revived group and then testing high is a plausible composition, but no inspected source settles the low-to-high boundary. Do not present it as a verified timing rule. Preserve both populations and block the divergent case pending the existing question.

If the player starts high and only the newly returned subset leaves, its native population cannot fall below its pre-return count unless another intervening action changes custody. This supplies an independently usable timing subset. Do not substitute old reserves for the returned group. A pending decision must reserve its exact eligible counters and revalidate intervening custody changes.

Tleilax specifies one destination. Southern does not explicitly settle whether a Basic group containing several stars can split among territories, nor whether repeated placements re-evaluate an entitlement earned by the group. The already-pending question covers that case. Do not resolve it through array iteration order or silently assert that plural wording mandates either one destination or several.

Sending the complete eligible group to one legal destination works under both candidate destination interpretations, so the splitting question does not require declining that common case. Neither face expressly permits an arbitrary strict subset through an up-to quantity. A complete eligible group or decline is the strongest directly supported quantity subset; do not present arbitrary partial deployment as separately verified. For Southern the eligible group means only its newly revived stars; for Tleilax it means only the ordinary free counters, excluding any paid counters returned alongside them. Finish that disposition once instead of repeatedly offering the remainder and thereby silently allowing splitting.

## Destinations and arrival effects

Homeworlds are not territories, and allied Homeworld destinations are forbidden. Southern's territory permission therefore excludes Homeworlds; Tleilax expressly includes them. Neither effect authorizes ignoring other occupancy restrictions. [E3, p.10](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf#page=10).

Southern's existing-force requirement is territorial, not a Great Flat distance limit. Its face supplies no express storm permission. The Fremen half-loss permission in the November FAQ is explicitly about Shipment; applying it to revival placement would be an additional interpretation. Fremen reserves are on-planet. Strongholds containing two other factions cannot be entered for any reason. [November FAQ, pp.2,5](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=5).

Use legal out-of-storm sectors for the independently supported Southern subset. Do not label storm placement definitely forbidden or definitely half-loss solely from that subset. Preserve ordinary alliance and protected-location rules; the word territory is not authority to bypass the Hidden Mobile Stronghold's source-specific restrictions.
