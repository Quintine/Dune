# Ordinary consumed-card continuation checkpoint

7 September 2026. Hajr, Ghola, Harvester, Weather Control and Family Atomics now retire through the shared saved-discard infrastructure after their existing effects have completed. Normal actions automatically drain the frame within the original action; recovery restores suspended controls without playing the card again. This checkpoint does not activate Semuta or broaden supported card timing, faction rules or expansion starts.

## Five completed effects, one restoration boundary

The live ordinary-card dispatcher retains its existing timing, selection, ownership and promise checks. Each accepted effect, its physical discard and its public played-card log are committed before staging. The new `ordinaryCardDiscard` continuation contains `player`, `card`, the explicit five-effect discriminant, the four suspended controls and `stateSignature`. The single public batch has cause `ordinary:<effect>` and the actual used card's former owner.

| Card            | Already committed before the frame                                                                                                                                | Recovery must preserve                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hajr            | The owner is added once to the movement allowance.                                                                                                                | Completed movement, the one extra allowance and the current movement queue; no third move or early turn ending.                                                                 |
| Weather Control | The validated selected distance, including zero, replaces `stormPending`; ready seats are cleared.                                                                | Original storm position, dial records and selected distance; storm movement waits for ordinary confirmation.                                                                    |
| Harvester       | The open fresh blow doubles, its Harvester count increases and readiness clears. Outside the storm, only the previous fresh-blow amount is added to ground spice. | The resulting blow and ground amounts; older ground spice is not multiplied, and no ground spice appears in the storm. A second physical Harvester creates its own later event. |
| Family Atomics  | The Wall is destroyed, all existing Wall force losses are applied through the existing casualty helper, and readiness clears.                                     | Ordinary and elite losses/tanks and surviving groups elsewhere; the owner may already have lost its qualifying Wall fighters.                                                   |
| Ghola           | The selected force, leader or Kwisatz Haderach revival, applicable technology accrual and Tleilaxu income response are established.                               | Exactly that revived state, leader custody/cycle, banked technology and unpaid income; no second revival, accrual or payment.                                                   |

The suffix marks the discard sequence resolved, clears the frame and restores `response`, `decision`, `pendingKarama` and `phaseOpening`. It does not replay the original action, repeat availability checks against the post-effect position, regenerate randomness or restore a whole older Game. Normal finalizers run only after restoration. A newly restored Tleilaxu income response may wait for its real cancellation opportunity or settle automatically under the existing rules.

## Ghola custody and earned income

Ghola's existing helper remains authoritative. Force revival transfers the chosen ordinary/elite counts once, preserving normal revival usage and the existing Ixian cyborg exception. An owned dead leader keeps its identity, original pool, death count and control record; only the existing revival changes occur. A controlled foreign leader remains in its original owner's leader array. Captured-leader denial and native-owner denial while foreign control persists remain unchanged. Kwisatz Haderach's revival cycle advances only in the actual Ghola effect, never during recovery.

The signature includes player leaders and control records, Kwisatz state, normal revival ledgers and the shared Duke Vidal record. Binding Duke Vidal preserves that authoritative state; it does not introduce a new Ghola eligibility rule. These receipts do not decide unresolved leader/revival-cycle interactions or grant foreign Auditor eligibility.

A seated Tleilaxu's Ghola reward is a separate one-spice `revivalIncome` response, including self-Ghola under the existing rule. The revived pieces remain revived whether that income is allowed or canceled. Ghola does not consume an already-recorded normal free-revival income allowance. Axlotl accrual is separate: where eligible in Revival, it banks the number of technology tokens held by its owner once that turn and pays at phase end. It is neither immediate cash nor the number of pieces revived.

For this ordinary-card source, the validator permits the expected Ghola income response and requires its exact Tleilaxu owner, Ghola recipient, amount one and initial empty passes. It rejects unexpected suspended decisions, Karama conversions or phase openings. The general continuation shape is not permission to insert arbitrary parents into an ordinary play.

## Saved-state validation and privacy

Shared validation requires the current playing-state discard sequence, turn/phase, one public physical receipt and unique card custody across real zones. The ordinary validator matches the exact player/card/effect, checks the resulting effect state and validates suspended controls without applying them. Its signature binds relevant movement, storm, spice, battle/promise, leader/revival, player resource and technology state. Private hands and seat AI control are excluded from the outcome signature and are never restored from it; normal card-custody checks still apply.

A signature is a saved-state consistency check, not authentication against coordinated rewriting of every bound value. The frame is internal evidence, not another physical copy of the card. Projection exposes the generic automatic-continuation marker and the already-public played-card log, while keeping receipt/signature and other seats' private state hidden. Shipment and battle preparation-completion projections remain suppressed while a discard frame is pending.

Production room recovery uses the existing compare-and-swap version fence. A pending discard reaches engine validation even if conflicting live controls or a finished status would otherwise make the scheduler wait. Corrupt frames reject before a write; duplicate workers can produce only one committed continuation. Retired sequences and stale original card plays cannot repeat the effect.

## Preparation searches remain simulations

Battle and shipment preparation use different paths. Battle preparation already calls the pure Ghola effect helper and performs trial discard accounting on its cloned position. That path remains free of live discard frames.

Shipment preparation previously called the ordinary inner dispatcher directly, so live frame staging would otherwise strand its hypothetical Ghola before later pledge withdrawal, shipping Karama or shipment. It now passes the explicit internal execution argument `shipmentPreparation`. The deterministic effect, card accounting and trial state remain equivalent, while the ordinary tail omits live frame staging. This argument belongs to the private function call; user action JSON cannot select it. Public card plays always use the live path.

The search does not run a general game normalizer, re-enter public action promise reconciliation or advance unrelated phases to complete a trial. Returned preparations remain owner-only, immutable during repeated reads and executable as genuine actions. Live Ghola frames restore their income context before ordinary promise reconciliation, so a conditional Tleilaxu support promise is not released merely because its response was temporarily suspended. Cancellation of the restored income can still release an infeasible promise under the existing rules.

## Focused verification and fixture limits

The new engine suite covers first-move Hajr, zero/pre-dial and post-dial Weather Control, successive Harvesters and storm loss, Atomics after its qualifying fighters die, ordinary/elite Ghola, native leaders, Kwisatz cycles, real foreign-Ghola acquisition followed by a documented later-death scenario, Ixian cyborg revival, technology and Tleilaxu income. It also checks conditional battle funding, a shipment requiring Ghola plus escrow withdrawal and Karama, all four AI profiles, public/private projections, corrupted outcomes and attempts to select the internal execution argument through action JSON. These are bounded engine scenarios, not full expansion games.

The five new SQLite tests in `tests/ordinary-card-discard-recovery.test.ts` passed in the focused run (6.01 seconds; `/tmp/dune-ordinary-card-discard-recovery.log`). They run real migrations and production room modules with authenticated room identities. Their explicit component positions use one physically sourced expanded deck and conserved force inventories. Actual movement and fresh-blow actions establish the relevant prerequisites; a test-only VM observes the production inner card dispatcher before automatic drain without adding a runtime export.

The SQL cases verify typed Ghola revival with two-token Axlotl accrual, actual income allowance/cancellation and later technology payout; Hajr after a real first move followed by exactly one extra move; Weather zero; two real Harvesters over a fresh blow and existing spice; and Atomics typed casualties. Concurrent recovery preserves committed resources, forces, cards and logs. Duplicate Ghola/Weather requests commit the complete effect and retirement within one original CAS. **Fifty-four malformed-frame variants** reject with zero writes, followed by retired-frame replay checks for all five effects. Named formatting and lint passed. These fixtures do not claim a publicly started Advanced expansion session, browser acceptance or complete faction-rule coverage.
