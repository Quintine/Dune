# Homeworld charity and revival integration audit

Read-only inspection, 9 September 2026. This document describes the source at inspection, not a completed implementation or a full Homeworld rules claim. Parent integration may supersede individual line numbers. No runtime or test files were changed for this audit.

## Verified scope and existing foundation

[HOMEWORLD_RULES.md](HOMEWORLD_RULES.md), based on the GF9 Ecaz/Moritani rulebook pp.9–10 and FAQ p.16, records a low native Homeworld's +1 free-revival rate and +1 **bank-paid** spice when collecting CHOAM Charity. Salusa supplies neither bonus. Karama cannot cancel Homeworld advantages or penalties. Tleilax's low face specifically conditions its loss of **other factions' free-revival income** on starting Revival at low population. These are different timing contracts.

`homeworld-population.ts:23–59` already derives public native population and potential bonuses from validated physical custody. It excludes visitors and Salusa's bonus, counts Salusa Sardaukar separately, and performs no payment. `homeworld-game.ts` validates physical inventory before public action, normalization and projection. Native reserve deposits now route revived Emperor normal forces to Kaitain and Sardaukar to Salusa. This physical foundation does not yet apply the economic benefits inspected below.

Population currently reflects physical counters, not the future occupation rule that can retain a low penalty. Reuse it for the released native population domain; do not claim that this completes occupied Homeworld effects.

## Normal free revival: shared rate, frozen request, ordinary limits

`revival.ts:42–91` provides the shared free remaining, force price and maximum request functions. Their present contexts do not include Homeworld custody. Introduce a public native-rate input or widen their declared context to include validated Homeworld state and public typed reserves. Preserve source provenance: native base/Fremen-alliance free rate, Homeworld addition, ordinary usage and any named restriction.

A low Homeworld increases the free **rate**, not a general paid-revival limit, global elite limit or available Tanks. The subsequent [source audit](HOMEWORLD_BENEFITS_RULES.md) confirmed E3's explicit four-free Fremen entitlement; the implementation uses the greater of ordinary capacity and current free rate to make that entitlement usable. `forceRevivalRemaining` also uses free remaining to enforce Fremen's no-paid-revival rule when no Tleilaxu is seated; it must receive the same new rate. Keep Emperor-funded extra returns, Ghola and leader revival separate.

**Named prohibition:** `revivalRules.freeBlocked` is CHOAM's **La La La**, set at `engine.ts:12476`, not a Karama cancellation flag. The independent source reviewer confirmed E2 p.7 prevents taking Free Revival; direct composition blocks the whole free rate, including the Homeworld addition. Homeworld Karama immunity provides no exception. Likewise, prevention of the entire normal revival is distinct from cancellation of an ordinary price advantage.

## Tleilax: persist the phase-start condition

`beginPhase` at `engine.ts:9176` initializes Revival rules and usage. Add one public, turn-bound phase-start receipt identifying the Tleilaxu seat and whether its native Tleilax was low then. A later ordinary revival or Ghola can increase population; neither refresh nor subsequent income may recompute the original starting condition. Reject malformed/stale new receipts and define a deliberate legacy policy rather than inventing historical population from the current board.

`openPhase` creates an Ix/Amal opening before `beginPhase`; the current ordinary opening permits Amal/pass, plus explicitly earlier card-transfer overlays. Those do not directly change reserves. Still bind the latch to the actual phase initialization once, preserve it through nested opening parents, and do not overwrite it during JSON recovery. Add it to any saved continuation signature whose next income depends on it.

There are **two income producers**, both needing one shared eligibility predicate:

- `revival-resume.ts:338–365` quotes normal settlement's paid transfer plus the first free-revival reward, and emits a per-payer `freeIncome` receipt.
- `engine.ts:11821 collectRevivalIncome` handles card/special routes including Ghola, with separate `free` and `ghola` flags.

Existing `revivalFreeIncome[player] = turn` only tracks whether a reward opportunity was consumed. It cannot encode Tleilax's starting population. Suppress only the source-supported other-faction free-revival reward. Preserve paid fees; do not blanket-disable `revivalIncome`, the Tleilaxu seat, or Ghola rewards because the current population is low. The source reviewer confirmed paid fees and the separate Ghola reward remain distinct from the specifically named other-faction Free Revival penalty; retain those routes rather than infer suppression from a boolean's name.

`terminal-cancellation.ts:163` deliberately permits Ghola's revival-income response outside phase4. Keep that domain. `viewGame` hides income amounts from seats other than owner/recipient (`engine.ts:18170`); the new public phase-start condition does not authorize exposing private payment totals. The `ordinaryCardDiscard` signature already includes Homeworld custody; any additional phase-start receipt used by its detached income suffix must also be bound.
