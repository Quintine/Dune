## Authority and selected interpretation

The [November2020 FAQ p8](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=8)
supplies general current-turn question/compliance/impossibility authority;
its previously checked indexed text is recorded in the
[ordinary-bid contract](TRUTHTRANCE_BID_PROMISES.md). A direct fetch during this
slice returned403, so it supplies no newly checked dedicated revival ruling.
The user-authorized Advanced source, physical39 replacement Truthtrance text,
also requires compliance during the current turn and permits later impossibility
release. That local passage was read for this slice. Original native revival
quotes and [Advanced turn order](ADVANCED_REVIVAL_ORDER.md) remain authoritative.

Under standing14October choose-recommended authority, select an explicit
**cumulative future ordinary-return** question. Count completed returns after
each definite answer. Splitting a return does not avoid No. Each elite is one
physical counter; normal means amount minus elites. Already completed returns,
leaders, Ghola, Emperor-funded extras and other special returns are excluded
because the question specifically names ordinary force revival. Actual native
requests, responses, quotes, force transfers and payments remain unchanged.

The phase interval/counting and accepted-order impossibility policy are
provisional software compositions, not a dedicated publisher clarification.
Waiting or current participants with a remaining opportunity can answer.
Basic No persists through phase close because readiness can reopen. An ordered
Advanced participant's No finishes when its native opportunity closes.
An unmet Yes prevents voluntary Finish Revival and spending or requests that
would remove the remaining completion. Completed opposing consequences can
release only impossible Yes answers, retaining No and feasible answers in
accepted order. A declaration alone never fulfills a promised return.

with its own event and waits for actual native turn ownership. Manual requests
retain their original shape. Stale guided events reject.
