**Prototyped / Partial.** The packet is
`{kind:'mixedMovement',target,turn,mixed}`. The raw tree combines current

## Authority and conservative snapshot meaning

Reuse the recorded [nonbattle source audit](TRUTHTRANCE_NONBATTLE_READINESS_20260907.md#source-basis),
[November2020 FAQp8](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf)
current-turn/AND/OR authority and supplied Advanced physical39/printed37 public
answer/compliance/release/unknown text. These sources do not prescribe an
online solver. The existing [mixed-shipment model](COMPOUND_SHIPMENT_PROMISES.md)
provides the current-fact snapshot/aggregate precedent. This conservative
composition remains explicit provisional software interpretation under
standing14October choose-recommended priority.

Facts freeze at actual answer time. A fact about holding Hajr stays the
answer-time fact after the same physical card is played and discarded. Later
spending/forces/custody do not create a promise to preserve the earlier state.
Only the complete yes/no answer is public. Individual fact answers and privately
reduced branches are not separately published.

For a definite Yes, unknown facts take the worst case false; for No, the worst
case true. The remaining owned-action completion must guarantee that entire selected
answer. The formula is positive AND/OR, so the conservative case guarantees the
aggregate for all unresolved fact values. This does not infer correlations
between unknown predicates. If neither definite answer has a known completion,
unknown/save/change-question remains available. No negates the whole question,
not each fact or action leaf.

True Hajr OR move can settle Yes immediately. False Hajr AND move can settle No
immediately. True Hajr AND move retains its future movement requirement;
unknown forecast OR move Yes must actually move, while unknown forecast AND
move No must avoid the qualifying group. Ordinary per-destination maximum
progress, not summed smaller moves, remains.

## Native integration and controls

`game/mixed-action-question.ts` validates the full public tree before any
short-circuit, with one16-leaf/depth4 budget including nested fact groups. The
server's existing fact decoder and exact movement-leaf decoder remain authority.
Public constants, targets, turns and other metadata cannot be forged inside
clauses. The reusable compiler evaluates all fact leaves before folding known
constants/identity children/singletons into an action-only residual.

The engine converts that residual into the existing movement-expression ledger;
there is one physical fulfillment ledger, not a second set of movement/payment
rules. Raw `mixedMovementQuestions` metadata links the original question to its
binding event. A constant aggregate needs no native binding. Existing owned
search, card preservation, preparations, funding, range responses and actual
owner finish retain their original behavior. Early readiness resets uniformly
for definite mixed answers; no public branch-specific readiness cue is added.
New mixed discard signatures include its metadata/binding while older record
signatures keep their original shape.

Only own raw question, aggregate answer/status and recorded native movement
maxima are projected. Compiled residuals are filtered out of the public pure
movement ledger, and raw internal metadata/frozen fact answers are not exposed.
The native guide is still entitled only to its owner and exact live event.
No separate public movement-fulfillment message reveals whether a private fact
created a residual. Required whole-answer release and public physical actions
retain their original aggregate information.

The full controlled editor reuses current-fact and nested-fact fields, supports
add/wrap/nest/switch/remove/collapse and preserves repairable invalid drafts.
Preview/Ask retain the whole raw tree. The aggregate panel and pure/scalar
movement panels show one shared exact guide. Four minimal policies answer and
follow that native guide; strategy/calibration remain deferred.
