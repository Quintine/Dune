# Ecaz Duke revival: production readiness

Read-only review, 7 September 2026. Recommend a shared-disc revival contract before adding a selectable Duke to ordinary revival or Ghola. The printed permission and price are clear; post-revival active custody and subsequent six-disc cycles remain material interpretation boundaries already recorded in [ECAZ_REVIVAL_RULES.md](ECAZ_REVIVAL_RULES.md). This review does not silently resolve them, enable full Ecaz starts, or certify all expansion rules.

## Verified rule boundary

Fresh official indexed retrieval confirms Ecaz can revive Duke at a normal price of five spice without a dead-leader threshold. Five Ecaz leaders in the Tanks, counting Duke, permit ordinary revival even with a survivor. Only Ecaz may revive Duke, including through Ghola. His battle strength remains six and he has no Traitor card. These are Basic Ecaz rules as well as Advanced rules. [GF9 E3, printed pp.8–9](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf#page=8)

The same clarification says Ecaz obtains Duke through its Ambassador and describes its tenure after acquisition. It does not expressly assign a controller after revival. Ordinary revived leaders are playable, supporting active Ecaz control as a composition; the competing reading leaves the living shared disc aside. This retrieval found no Duke-specific answer choosing between them. Do not spend five spice or a Ghola and quietly decide whether the resulting leader can be used. [GF9 E3, p.9](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/EcazMoritani-Rulebook-LOWRES.pdf#page=9), [GF9 Quick Start, p.7](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/Quick-start-Guide.pdf#page=7)

Normal revival grants one leader per faction per Revival phase; Tleilaxu early revival does not add slots. Its allied leader discount halves the normal price, rounded up. Thus applying that existing discount to Duke gives three spice, with cancellation restoring five rather than six. Payment remains the reviver’s responsibility; Tleilaxu cannot pay for its ally. These are compositions of existing powers, not a Duke-specific discount example. [GF9 November 2020 FAQ, p.6](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=6), [GF9 Ixians & Tleilaxu, p.7](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf#page=7)

Local corroboration: publisher-authored `/tmp/dune-rules/ecaz-audit.txt:329–331,349–365`, `/tmp/dune-rules/base.txt:511–527,1347–1351`, and `/tmp/dune-rules/ix-official-mirror.txt:250–270`. E3 mirror provenance is recorded in [ECAZ_AMBASSADOR_EFFECTS_AUDIT.md](ECAZ_AMBASSADOR_EFFECTS_AUDIT.md). Search crawl/publish metadata is not a rules revision date. No forum or tournament rule was adopted.

## Minimal shared contract and integration order

1. **Implement the unambiguous identity/eligibility foundation.** A pure shared resolver accepts native leader IDs or the canonical shared Duke and returns an explicit source discriminator. Duke requires a seated Ecaz reviver, one canonical physical disc, dead state, valid history, and no unresolved capture/ghola/concealed record or duplicate native copy. Missing Ecaz means nobody may revive him. Preserve the existing Advanced Harkonnen/exotic-custody support boundary uniformly, rather than exposing private availability differences.
2. **Expose the known ordinary quote without inventing a new transaction kind.** `leaderRevivalOptions` can add a Duke choice with `normalCost:5`, discounted cost, `early:false`, affordability and source `shared`. `{type:'reviveLeader',leader:'duke-vidal'}` can retain `PendingRevival.kind:'leader'` after its resolver is widened. The request uses the existing ordinary slot, turn/phase/prevention checks and actual Tleilaxu stop opportunity. Queue only the existing discount check when relevant; fewer dead native leaders must not produce an invented early-revival permission check.
3. **Make post-revival custody an explicit resolved input before activating payment.** A proposed pure outcome may take a required internal policy enum `activeEcaz | setAside`, with no default and no user-submitted bypass. Once the supported interpretation is selected, return the full next `DukeState`, preserving ID, strength, death history and applicable battle-use metadata. If active Ecaz is selected, persist explicit shared controller/tenure rather than inserting the disc into `p.leaders`. Do not automatically claim the acquisition helper’s `source:'ecaz'` semantics resolve this revival question.
4. **Compose existing accounting, do not copy a force revival.** Ordinary paid Duke sets `leaderRevived=true`, spends its frozen price once, leaves force/free-force/elite counters unchanged and routes the existing Tleilaxu income response. `free:0` produces no CHOAM free-force interception or Axlotl free-force accrual. Discount cancellation must validate the shared source before the cost and later allowance, with unchanged five-spice normal price. Preserve unfunded fizzle and actual manual stop boundaries.
5. **Implement the separate Ghola branch with the same identity/custody outcome.** Require Ecaz even when the old temporary controller is Moritani. Do not require ordinary leader-cycle eligibility or spend the ordinary leader slot: current Ghola does neither. Preserve its existing legal card windows and its independent Tleilaxu one-spice income behavior. Current Ghola calls `techIncome('axlotl', actor)` for leader as well as force returns; compose and test the existing module behavior rather than silently deleting it. The existing consumed-card frame binds the complete outcome. Update both real execution and speculative preparation candidates together.
6. **Treat the Ecaz five-disc threshold as a separate cycle change.** Start with explicit first-cycle examples and persist the opened eligibility; do not recheck raw dead count after each return. A separate cohort/eligibility record can avoid appending Duke to a native roster. Its repeated-cycle transition policy must be established before activation across arbitrary games; a minimum of six death counters is not a sourced replacement for that policy.

The safe immediate production improvement without either unresolved interpretation is the canonical **Ecaz-only Ghola rejection guard**, including synthetic retained foreign-control saves, plus reusable shared-disc identity/price quoting kept behind the existing activation boundary. The five-dead-native-count defect can be developed with explicit first-cycle tests independently, but should not silently enable an invented general cycle algorithm.

## UI and AI contract

Ordinary UI at `components/game-table.tsx:3283–3335` already uses `g.revival.leaders` and submits IDs. Adding an authoritative Duke option should suffice for the core selector; its disabled explanation should distinguish used ordinary slot, insufficient spice, prevention and unfinished shared-custody interpretation. Keep battle strength six visible separately from price five. Update the Duke inspector’s revival coverage only after actual commit/recovery is supported.

Ghola UI at `components/game-table.tsx:4320–4365` uses dead `me.leaders` filtered by `controlsLeader`; it needs a shared authoritative `gholaLeaderOptions` list or equivalent explicit Ecaz Duke option. Native/foreign negotiation UI in `components/revival-commerce.tsx` must not accidentally offer Duke as an ordinary Tleilaxu foreign ghola or mandatory negotiated request. All projections must preserve non-owner hand/traitor privacy and give one generic unavailable descriptor for concealed custody.

`game/bots.ts:2289–2455` consumes authoritative normal revival options, so all four profiles can use a new legal Duke choice automatically. Its early-request and foreign-ghola enumerators still inspect projected leaders and need explicit shared-disc exclusions. There is no ordinary stand-alone Ghola-selection branch in current `bots.ts`; finite battle/shipment preparation witnesses can supply card actions in their own contexts. Add a bounded strategic Ghola choice using the same own-view eligible IDs if this feature is intended to be exercised in ordinary full games. Do not infer dead-Duke availability from an opponent’s hidden leader fields.

## Required evidence and remaining rulings

Before release, test genuine acquisition/battle death → paid revival at five or allied three → cancellation to five, paid-once and native-slot exclusion, current Tleilaxu stop/prevention and income; Basic/Advanced, absent Ecaz, missing/duplicate shared disc and every other faction’s Ghola rejection. Exercise Ghola outside Revival and in a real Battle preparation, its consumed-card JSON/SQLite recovery, all four AI profiles, truthful option projection, and a browser selector/inspector check.

Cycle tests need four dead natives plus Duke, five dead natives with Duke living, continuation after the first return, a persistent sixth survivor, repeated Duke deaths, first death of a late survivor after another cohort has progressed, and the captured-native unavailability exception. Do not count a living unavailable Duke as actually in the Tanks. A read-only quote cannot advance a cycle.

Open decisions remain: post-revival active controller/tenure, precise repeated six-disc cycle transitions, the existing cross-territory `usedAt` interaction when Ghola revives Duke during Battle, and whether Ecaz’s special revival advantage itself has a separate generic Karama cancellation. The E3 table has no dedicated Ecaz Revival row; that absence is not proof of immunity. Existing discount, prevention and income response families remain independent. Ambassador Tleilaxu revival, allied loans, exceptional capture returns and Occupy are separate unfinished features.
