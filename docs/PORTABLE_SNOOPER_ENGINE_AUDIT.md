# Portable Snooper integration audit

Audit: 2026-09-06. Scope: the canonical card's ordinary defense role, late play and existing battle continuations. The source contract below informed the bounded integration recorded at the end. The current-hook list is historical; the final checkpoint records completed implementation. Full expansion certification remains unverified.

## Primary evidence

The photographed publisher face is **Defense – Poison** and protects the leader against poison. Its extra permission is to play after revealing the Battle Plan when no defense was played, subject to Bene Gesserit Voice. It separately prohibits play after a plan containing both a weapon and a Worthless card. Its final instruction is the usual discard-after-use wording. The actual face was visually read at `/tmp/dune-rules/choam-snooper-reading.png`, enlarged from the [physical component photograph](https://cdn.anyfinder.eu/assets/QsUJtVktEC87xwK08oLlwnKyaozUWfuaLzG0LFEn67ENEEVgNrDGoITy3608Dqe7) linked by the [gallery](https://www.tabletopfinder.eu/en/boardgame/32692/dune-choam-richese). The local photograph and its hash are recorded in `RICHESE_ACQUISITION_RULES.md`; uploader printing/date is unverified. The face, not retailer commentary, is the evidence.

The [GF9 CHOAM/Richese FAQ, p10](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/CHOAM-Rulebook-low-res.pdf#page=10), explicitly distinguishes Portable Snooper from Snooper for CHOAM duplicate exchange. The [November 2020 FAQ, pp1 and8](https://www.gf9games.com/dune/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Dune-FAQ-Nov-2020.pdf#page=8), gives the general winner-retention rule, specific Artillery/used-Tooth disposal exceptions, and Chemistry-only protection against Poison Tooth. Publisher-indexed text was freshly retrieved for these statements. Bounded official searches found no Portable-specific answer overriding retention or placing its declaration relative to traitor/Tooth decisions.

The [base rules, pp10–11,18 and23](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/Dune-Rulebook.pdf) supply ordinary leader/hero and battle-card limits, simultaneous public plan reveal, traitor resolution, Voice constraints and unchanged inspected plan elements. These general rules compose with the extra timing permission; they do not convert the card into a late-only Special action.

## Confirmed composition and practical contract

**Ordinary plan use is supported.** Its printed category is a poison defense; the late permission is an additional option, not a prohibition on selecting it as the original defense. Support both Basic and Advanced battles without changing the physical card identity. The current inventory intentionally stores it as `kind:'special'`; use an exact-canonical role adapter rather than rewriting persisted cards into ordinary Snooper IDs/kinds. An unverified forged `effect:'portableSnooper'` is insufficient authority.

**Late use adds protection without editing the revealed plan.** Begin only after the public battle reveal, not the private advanced Atreides inspection of one sealed plan. Keep the original weapon, defense, leader, dial and support immutable. Record the additional played card separately. A real defense in the original plan blocks this extra defense; a lone Worthless card is not actual protection and the separate weapon-plus-Worthless ban supports adding Portable alongside it. This includes the engine's lone Worthless stored in its defense field. Respect the ordinary two-card weapon/defense limit: two existing Worthless cards leave no third card space. A leader or Cheap Hero must still have been played.

**Voice still applies.** A poison-defense prohibition applies to this late defense. A poison-weapon prohibition does not forbid a defense merely because both involve poison. Ordinary Voice compulsion must already have been met in the sealed plan when possible: a holder cannot omit its only compulsory poison defense and promise to add Portable later. When compulsion was impossible, or required another category, it does not independently prohibit an otherwise legal late defense. A compelled lone Worthless can coexist with Portable subject to the card limits. Reuse the actual Voice match functions, including canonical poison-defense role recognition, rather than checking card names or only `kind:'snooper'`.

**Damage and disposal stay under ordinary battle precedence.** Portable stops an ordinary poison weapon or Chemistry used as a poison weapon. It only supplies the poison half of defense against Poison Blade; the projectile half still kills an otherwise unprotected leader. It is not a shield, does not create or stop a Lasgun–shield explosion, does not stop Lasgun or Artillery, and does not stop Poison Tooth. A successful traitor declaration still decides the battle before ordinary weapon effects. Existing Tooth activation remains a genuine choice; Portable does not override its exception. Winning players may retain a used Portable under the general FAQ rule, including when played late. A losing player discards it, except an eligible Moritani allied-retention choice. Normal mutual-loss/explosion cleanup discards it. No retrieved specific exception makes this card mandatory-discard for a winner. Do not mistake the generic face disposal sentence for the special Artillery/Tooth erratum.

These are general-rule compositions with explicit provenance, not invented Portable-specific FAQs. Existing unsupported Mirror Weapon/Stone Burner or other future card combinations remain outside this slice until their own effects exist; they do not prevent ordinary poison-defense support.

## Opportunity and source timing boundary

The text grants late use but does not order that declaration before/after traitor calls or Poison Tooth activation. Preserve current battle-effect precedence regardless of the input sequence. A new decision appearing only when somebody holds Portable would disclose possession. Automatically passing only nonholders produces a timing oracle as well.

The accepted minimal application contract reuses each combatant's existing genuine traitor-declaration opportunity. Before submitting their own `traitorCall`, they may use the separate Portable action. Both combatants must already submit their traitor decisions before battle resolution, so an opponent's fast request cannot eliminate this opportunity. No additional acknowledgment or holder-only public stage is needed. After the actor's own traitor declaration, reject late use as a stale relinquished opportunity. The private control should make that consequence clear. This is an explicit asynchronous scheduling convention, not an assertion that the publisher mandates Portable-before-own-traitor-call. Current Tooth choices finish before traitor declarations; their decision must continue to block unrelated mutations. Keep genuine Harkonnen allied traitor/Karama response continuations intact.

Do not automatically play Portable just because it would save a leader, and do not require a redundant global ready stage. Declaring or withholding a held defense is strategic. Opponents see no possession/eligibility metadata; the card and its public extra protection appear only after actual play. A private blocker may explain the holder's own hand/plan/Voice conditions. A public outcome already decided by a traitor does not make Portable a shield or change precedence; do not use that fact to reclassify its card identity or trigger unrelated effects.

## Current engine work required

1. **Role everywhere:** add exact `isPortableSnooper` and shared `isDefenseCard` handling in `battle-cards.ts`; `defenseTypes` gives poison-defense semantics while `isShield` remains false. Update `validBattleCardPair`, plan legality, all defense search/filter loops, Voice availability, Prescience feasibility, bots and the battle editor. A normal Portable defense also satisfies Chemistry's need for another defense when Chemistry is a weapon. Keep physical canonical matching for CHOAM unchanged.
2. **Truth and inspection:** `battle-promises.ts::parsePlanClaim` currently whitelists only `treacheryDeck(['ix'])`, and `truthtrance.ts::TRUTH_CARD_NAMES` has the same limitation. Add the supported canonical card name to battle-plan and hand-fact choices, including actual editor options. An ordinary Portable defense must be reachable when answering a structured promise. Existing structured claims describe the original slot and therefore remain evaluated against the immutable sealed plan; a promised empty original defense can coexist with this explicit later card permission. A genuinely broader freeform promise about never playing a defense remains broader than that slot claim and must not be silently advertised as machine-enforced by this model.
3. **Separate state and event:** use `Battle.lateDefense?: Record<playerId,cardId>` and the current battle event. Do not replace `plans[player].defense`, especially a Worthless card or an inspected null. Effective combat defense can consult the overlay; original plan display/Prescience/full-plan insight must distinguish original commitment from the later public card.
4. **Commitment custody:** keep the late card in its owner's hand as an explicitly played/reserved battle card, matching existing plan-card custody. Extend `transferCardBlock`, direct discard/exchange/cash-in reservations and physical played-card enumeration to include it. Both outgoing-card checks in Distrans, paused Richese gifts and saved accepted Ix replacements must reject competing use. Central paid-Box and Truthtrance locks retain priority; late play cannot overwrite a pending decision/response. No new randomness, fee, bank payment or faction-income window is needed.
5. **Resolution and cleanup:** `resolveBattle` currently fetches defense only from each plan. Supply the effective defense to ordinary effects, and add late card IDs to the `used` set. The same set must feed losing-player disposal, winner cleanup and `moritaniRetention.played/eligible`; otherwise the card can escape disposal or incorrectly disappear before the retention decision. Deduplicate physical IDs. Keep Poison Tooth, explosion, leader death, bounty, casualties, capture, Face Dance and technology continuations in their existing order.
6. **Recovery and bot behavior:** validate actor, exact card, battle event, both-plan reveal, own traitor-declaration status and current custody on every request. Old saved battles without an event cannot accept a forged new event; an explicit compatibility policy may keep them unsupported without rewriting sealed state. Bots should consider their private Portable option before their own traitor declaration and honor source restrictions. Private preview must not mutate, resolve damage or draw RNG.

## Accepted action/projection shape

```ts
{ type: 'portableSnooper', card: canonicalPortableId, event: battleEvent }
Battle.lateDefense?: Record<string, string>;

// Held-card option: owner only. Public battle gets lateDefense after actual use.
portableSnooper: null | { card: Card; event: string | null; blocked: string | null };
```

On valid use, reserve the held physical card, record the public late defense and log the declaration once. Do not rewrite the sealed plan, auto-call a traitor, award income, discard immediately or resolve the battle prematurely. Normal winner/loser cleanup decides its final physical destination. The engine's immutable action clone and room CAS reject duplicate/stale requests without another record or disposal.

- Late empty slot and lone Worthless in either stored slot; weapon-plus-Worthless, two Worthless and existing genuine defense rejection; original plan remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Actual Voice compel/prohibit/default-role behavior; original null or Worthless Prescience answer preserved; real structured Portable-name Truth question and reachable plan; no promise silently reinterpreted as a changed original slot.
- Fast opponent traitor request cannot resolve before the holder acts; own completed declaration closes the option; active Tooth/Truth/Harkonnen response preserves ownership and resumes normally; no public hand-possession oracle.
- Ordinary winner retain/discard, loser disposal, successful/double traitor, explosion, Moritani ally retention, advanced casualties and subsequent capture/Face Dance/technology all account for one late physical card exactly once.
- JSON/restart, public revealed-late versus private unused card, authenticated concurrent late use and traitor submission, stale event/duplicate action, no random projection work, and four AI levels.

No additional user question was sent. The declaration scheduling convention and any broader freeform promise scope are recorded honestly; the ordinary role, Voice checks, damage, separate commitment state and standard retention are concrete implementation work.
