# Ecaz territorial occupancy checkpoint

7 September 2026. This implements territorial co-occupation during movement within the existing gated Ecaz development scope. Full Ecaz, Advanced and expansion acceptance remain unfinished. [The source contract](ECAZ_OCCUPY_RULES.md) and [pre-change runtime review](ECAZ_OCCUPY_RUNTIME_READINESS.md) distinguish this rule from combined combat, collection and victory.

## Behavior

Ecaz and its reciprocal ally count as one occupying faction when entering or passing through a board stronghold. They may share a territory permanently. Ordinary shipment, ground movement, Guild transport and the dedicated Fremen/Guild Ambassador destinations now use one public occupancy calculation. A third unrelated occupying faction is still excluded; BG advisors do not fill a stronghold. A concealed No-Field supplies public presence without exposing or reading its denomination.

Ending movement no longer sends a co-occupying Ecaz partner's forces to the Tanks merely because the ally already completed its turn. The table explains retained co-occupation in the chronicle and emits the existing optional Ecaz-coloured Occupy notice. Other alliances retain their existing departure rule. Shipment costs, physical ownership, elite allocation, movement limits, storm, mobile-stronghold admission and arrival interruptions keep their own checks. Explicit homeworld identity never inherits the territorial exception.

BG's established advisors may request fighters beside an Ecaz ally when the ordinary conditions allow it, including the start-of-movement battle preparation opportunity. New accompaniment locks remain binding. The AI uses the same occupancy checks for ordinary and marker destinations, ground paths and Ornithopter paths; its destination scoring no longer eliminates Ecaz's legal allied targets before candidate selection.

## Deliberate boundaries

This is not complete Occupy. Combined battle selection, dial ownership, support, casualty allocation, shared desert allocation, Collection cancellation and the three-co-occupied victory condition still require their own integration. The source audit's proposed numerical correction was found to be a community comment, not a designer ruling; the document now records the unresolved printed rounding conflict accurately. The separately verified designer explanation of territorial entry remains applicable.

No new mode gate was opened. These tests and the staged browser position do not establish a complete Ecaz or expansion game, nor AI strength calibration. The full application goal remains active.
