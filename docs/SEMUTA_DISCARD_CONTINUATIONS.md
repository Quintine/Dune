# Semuta discard producers and continuation inventory

Read-only engine audit, 2026-09-07. Read the complete `docs/SEMUTA_DRUG_ENGINE_AUDIT.md`, then inspected every current `discard(...)` call, direct Treachery-discard mutation and `draw(g)` caller in `game/engine.ts`, plus the Truthtrance discard callback and pure Box/Distrans results. Names below are integration anchors; approximate current lines are navigation aids only. This report adopts no additional source ruling and changes no project file.

## Printed special activation and cashout stages — 16 October 2026

`specialKaramaDiscard` retains the validated original native intent, cost,
controls and signature. The original activation pays its cost once; the
authenticated saved discard can invoke the single native effect body with
that committed canonical card even after it moves to the claimant.

- `effect`: the native pre-effect printed cost is fresh; resume its original
  selection/permission/prevention/purchase/movement once.
- `complete`: native Emperor revival and its original ledger already
  committed before disposal. Retire the cost event without another return.
- `cashout`: CHOAM's selected hand cards are one private simultaneous batch
  after the original credit. Commit before inspection, choose one fresh
  other-player card, and never repeat credit or include the earlier cost.

Original joint Richese/Nexus cost ordering, once-use, original typed source,
refund and queued parent remain native. The source signature binds outcomes
and obligations, not a stale replacement Game. Existing paid purchase-income
fixtures and price-stop consumers now issue actual neutral passes before
their native suffix rather than assuming direct completion.

[Scoped source/runtime proof](SEMUTA_SOURCE_UPDATE_20260907.md#printed-native-special-costs-and-choam-cashout--16-october-2026)
distinguishes eight original and eighteen controlled paths plus two original/
two controlled mobile journeys. Advanced E3 entry gates, converted costs
and full assurance remain separate.


## Shipment-price activation cost — 16 October 2026

`karamaShipmentPriceDiscard` offers the exact public printed cost before
activating its original other-player price. The unused opportunity,
beneficiary, creator and cost identity stay bound. Claim/decline creates the
same grant once; no shipping payment or counter departure occurs at this
boundary. The physical claimant does not become the benefit's creator.

After a claim, the grant records `costClaimed: true`. The selected extension
to the provisional Guild-stop refund policy does not return a claimed cost,
even if its new holder later discards it again as the special-stop cost.
The same physical card can pay that new legal use, but is not also refunded
to its former owner. An unclaimed cost retains its older exact-card refund.
This is source/nonduplication composition, not a publisher worked example.

[Current source and scoped proof](SEMUTA_SOURCE_UPDATE_20260907.md#shipment-price-cost-and-claimed-card-refund--16-october-2026)
records234 affected cases, fourteen original runtime/policy paths, two
controlled extra-card refund probes and four actual mobile journeys.
Converted costs and other special-Karama disposal sources remain unfinished;
full Semuta/module certification and final assurance are not implied.


## Native Guild cancellation cost family — 16 October 2026

`karamaShipmentDiscard` now suspends a printed cancellation cost for original
native permission/rate responses. Pending reserve, cross/return or typed
Homeworld frames remain in their own custody while the response is parked.
Existing response collectors see that saved response; an admitted Semuta
claim or all-seat decline restores it and finishes the cancellation once.

Unlike the already-paid income family, no shipment payment or physical
departure has happened. Recovering the cost cannot create a route, keep
half price, finance the unfunded group or repeat a delivery. Signature and
existing native validators bind the original group, approved split, source
books, allowance and any independent price grant. Ordinary permission and
affordability interpretations are unchanged.

The [current source/runtime checkpoint](SEMUTA_SOURCE_UPDATE_20260907.md#native-guild-permission-and-rate-costs--16-october-2026)
records158 affected cases,32 original runtime/policy paths and four actual
mobile cancellation/continuation journeys. These are changed-rule proofs,
not a new broad save/privacy/custody campaign or complete-mode acceptance.
Shipment-price activation disposal and special-stop refund composition
remain separate work.

## Main findings

A post-`applyAction` pile diff cannot implement immediate Semuta. Ix Ambassador and Ixian ally replacement discard then draw directly. Ordinary Karama cancellation/purchase can reach replacement, Harkonnen bonus or a new auction pool indirectly. A stopped frame must resume at a named stage, never rerun its original public action.

There are three direct real discard-pile producers outside `discard`: `finishNullentropy` installs the pure result pile containing the newly used Box; `finishOrnithopter` pushes its owned escrow card; the Distrans branch pushes only the used Distrans. `draw` drains/shuffles the pile but creates no new discard. Setup resets and trial-state discards are not live reaction events.

For each batch retain actual former owner per entry, exact physical ID, a face snapshot as evidence (not extra custody), and whether the face is already public. The initiator may differ from the discarder. Private batch faces cannot appear in public `decision`, `response`, logs or event payloads before an entitled committed inspection.

## Draw/refill consumers to guard

All actual Treachery draws funnel through `draw(g)` (~1795). It currently blocks only a paid Box; an unresolved Semuta batch needs an equivalent guard against refill/drain. Direct consumers are `dealStartingTreachery`; `setAuction`'s pool loop; Ix Ambassador replacement; Robbery; `finishResponse('ixAuction')`'s multi-card pool loop; `finishResponse('ixAllyCard')`; `finishResponse('harkonnenBonus')`; and the Ix setup decision's Harkonnen starting bonus. Setup normally cannot have a fresh Semuta discard, but the guard should remain universal.

Cancellation/purchase chain: `completeKarama` → `finishResponse` or `settleAuction` → `continueAuctionSale` → `auctionBonus`/`nextAuction` → Richese next-lot or phase preparation. Preserve `currentAuctionSale`, original payment, lot/card index, `free`, and pending Ix replacement across that chain. A future multi-draw continuation must store already drawn cards plus remaining count if it can itself suspend; restarting a draw loop from zero duplicates cards. No current raw `draw(g)` performs a discard itself, so well-placed discard pauses can usually leave these loops indivisible.

`finishAmbassador` may replenish five tokens using RNG; `finishBattle` can open retention, CHOAM income, tech, capture, Auditor, FaceDance or the next phase; `endMovement` may start a new phase; `finishChoamMarket` advances storm/phase; Fremen worm restoration resumes older phase state. None may run ahead merely because Semuta is represented by a field that the current automatic-finalization loop does not know.

## Exclusions, reservations and privacy hazards

- **Not discards:** Ix Technology substitutes hand and auction-pool cards; Ix auction selection returns a pool card to deck; unused auction cards return to deck; CHOAM allied swaps; Richese gifts; Harkonnen equal exchange; Sabotage gift; cache purchase; cache remove-from-game; normal purchase; Box's recovered target and shuffled old pile; Spice/Traitor/FaceDance/Nexus/leader/token movement. Do not emit events from generic hand disappearance or pile replacement.
- **Reserved rate card:** `karamaShipping.card` points to a physically discarded card which Guild special currently returns provisionally. BG conversion can carry this binding earlier inside `pendingKarama.use`; if pausing immediately after the cost, the saved `KaramaUse` already owns that future reservation before `karamaShipping` exists. An exclusion/escrow rule must inspect the suspended intent, not only the live top-level rate field. Do not silently settle the underlying refund ambiguity by giving the same card to two owners.
- **Other reserved cards:** Truthtrance queue heads, paid Box, committed gifts, Black Market offerings, Moritani retained cleanup and Ornithopter escrow each have existing ownership rules. Semuta can target only a completed discard, not a still-held reserved card. Preserve reserved incoming hand slots from auctions and mandatory exchanges when checking Semuta's user-approved atomic full-hand exchange; the final hand must still fit those obligations.
- **Trial engines:** `findReachableBattlePlan` discards Ghola on `trial`; special-intent and shipment-completion searches call effect executors on clones. These must not produce real Semuta prompts, consume RNG just to enumerate options, or treat a newly paused effect as already earned income. Give search execution an explicit internal simulation path that retains physical accounting without public interactive side effects.
- **Finalizer fences:** add pending-discard/claim checks before `finishActionContinuations`, both promise reconciliations and `settleAutomaticContinuations`; also room recovery/bot scheduling and top-level Truthtrance/Box handling. Otherwise temporary suspension can release a feasible promise, answer the next queued question, settle a payout or draw before Semuta. Once a batch closes, resume its typed suffix then ordinary finalization exactly once.
- **Integrity before visibility:** do not publish a battle halfway through mandatory cleanup: existing Auditor/Stone/late-defense invariants require coherent completed contexts. Similarly, do not leave a consumed Truthtrance at a live validated queue head or a discarded Ix purchase inside a validator requiring it still held. Move ownership to a stage-specific continuation before opening the neutral reaction.
