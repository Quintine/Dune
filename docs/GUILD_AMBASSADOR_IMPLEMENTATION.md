## Rules and bounded behavior

[GUILD_AMBASSADOR_RULES.md](GUILD_AMBASSADOR_RULES.md) records the primary sources and unresolved combinations. E3 pp.8,15 establishes the reserve grant and immediate shipment before the original entrant continues. The beneficiary may select one through four physical forces, including any required or chosen elites, and a clear destination sector. An explicit zero-force choice is implemented as the reading of “up to four”; the triggered token remains committed. No available reserves or board destination completes without moving forces under the documented unavailable-effect interpretation. An implementation-blocked arrival is not silently treated as a rules-impossible destination.

The pure quote preserves typed custody and validates board locations, storm, stronghold occupancy and reciprocal Ecaz Occupy. Fremen can enter outside their ordinary reinforcement radius, but cannot invoke their ordinary storm exception. Their classic southern reserves remain on-planet. Only Ixians may ship directly into their placed Hidden Mobile Stronghold. A direct BG shipment joins its existing advisors; otherwise it uses normal fighter arrival, without an invented movement-style flip choice.

The grant costs no spice: no bank/Guild transfer, pledge spending or ordinary rate-card consumption. It leaves ordinary shipment/movement flags, Hajr, queues, Guild timing, Sapho and the original entrant's Ornithopter cohort intact. It is separate from existing Basic-only Truthtrance shipment promises.

During phase five, an eligible Guild special Karama can prevent an off-planet declaration before placement. The exact intent survives the response. Stopping it returns to the Ambassador continuation without spending the beneficiary's ordinary shipment or altering an unrelated rate benefit. No phase-one Guild stop window is opened. Successful eligible off-planet shipments accrue Heighliner income once in its own phase, without requiring a payment. A Guild beneficiary alone does not trigger it, but a separate successful BG shipment can. Fremen reserve entry does not trigger these off-planet consequences.

The original entrant remains suspended while supported Intrusion, Terror and BG accompaniment resolve. BG receives its own sector/Polar Sink choices and may decline. [E1 p.10](https://www.gf9games.com/dunegame/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/IxianAndTleilaxuRulebook.pdf) explicitly permits BG accompaniment into an Ixian shipment's HMS; this does not authorize independent BG direct shipment there. November FAQ p.4 governs same-territory advisor stance and existing locks. A secondary arriving fighter can trigger another Ambassador: the completed Guild effect transfers its original continuation to that new opportunity, preserving the original worm rider where applicable. The shipment and token completion are not replayed.

The durable receipt validates historical actor, event, token, quantity and child provenance rather than requiring old forces, alliances or storm positions to be restored after later effects. Printed and BG-converted Karama use the same source bindings. Cancellation of an accompanying power preserves the already completed main shipment. Separate Terror can act on either main or accompanying arrivals. The final child finishes before the entrant or remaining worm ride resumes.

## Controls and AI

Only the beneficiary receives `ambassadorEntry.shipment`. The dedicated event-keyed form shows physical count, elite minimum/maximum, destination territory/sector, zero cost and an explicit **Send no forces** action. Server-projected blocked reasons disable submission and are associated with the controls. This dated physical-only baseline is extended by the Basic No-Field controls above.

All four AI profiles choose a positive projected legal shipment when available, preserve canonical elite allocation and otherwise choose zero. They filter blocked destinations before scoring. Offer/copy preference uses only the bot's own reserve availability, not an ally's hidden resources. For a source-bound BG follow-up, only its owner receives `guildAmbassadorAdvisorChoices`; UI and AI use the supplied sectors and blocking reasons. The AI falls back to Polar Sink or decline. Legacy accompaniment controls retain their prior behavior.

## Remaining boundaries

- **No-Field:** the24 October Basic prototype above supersedes the original implementation gate. Native Advanced and optional combined profiles remain gated; the combined cap and fee policy is provisional, without a direct publisher ruling.
- Simultaneous foreign-BG Intrusion/Terror and Ambassador/Terror remain explicit arrival gates. A rejected declaration commits no forces. The supported sequential or secondary cases do not resolve those competing priorities.
- Moritani Atomics Aftermath's shipment prohibition still requires its gated effect producer/state. Homeworlds, Discovery locations and their component modifiers require their own gated modules. The physical quote is not evidence that these absent combinations have been implemented.
- Tleilaxu Ambassador, complete Occupy combat/collection/victory, exceptional Duke custody and full optional-module combinations remain unfinished. The source-version conflict about Occupy combat rounding is unchanged.
